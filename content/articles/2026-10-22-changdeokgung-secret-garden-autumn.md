If you only have time for one palace garden in Seoul this autumn, make it **창덕궁** (Changdeokgung). Behind its halls lies **후원** (huwon), a royal garden of ponds, pavilions and old trees on a wooded hillside, known in English as the Secret Garden. From late October to mid-November the maples there turn orange and red, and it's one of the best places in the city to see **단풍** (danpung), autumn leaves. Details below are as of October 2026.

## Why Changdeokgung is different

Changdeokgung was built in the early 1400s as a secondary palace, but many Joseon kings preferred to live here. Unlike the straight, formal lines of other palaces, its buildings follow the shape of the hills, which is one reason it is listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The palace grounds are a **[궁궐](/en/dictionary/?q=궁궐)** (gunggwol), a royal palace complex, full of courtyards to wander. Look for **낙선재** (Nakseonjae), a group of unpainted wooden buildings in a quieter style, which were among the last homes of the royal family.

## The Secret Garden: tickets and tours

The garden can only be visited on a timed tour, so plan this first.

- **Tickets**: as of October 2026, the Secret Garden ticket costs 5,000 won for adults and 2,500 won for children, on top of the palace entry ticket of 3,000 won.
- **Booking**: each tour has a fixed number of places. Half are sold online in advance and half on site on the day, and online places go quickly on autumn weekends.
- **Tours**: the standard tour takes about an hour, with commentary in Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese. From March to November, English tours start at 10:30, 11:30, 14:30 and 15:30. Arrive at least 20 minutes early: late entry is not allowed.

The highlights are **부용지** (Buyongji), a square pond with a small pavilion standing in the water, and **애련지** (Aeryeonji), the "lotus-loving pond." Deeper in, **옥류천** (Ongnyucheon) is a stream where kings once floated wine cups along a carved rock channel. After the tour you can stay in the palace for the rest of the day.

## Hours, closing day and free Wednesdays

- **Hours**: 09:00–18:00 in October and 09:00–17:30 from November to January, with last entry one hour before closing.
- **Closed**: Mondays (if a Monday is a public holiday, the palace closes on the next weekday instead).
- **Free Wednesdays**: from October 2026, general palace entry at Changdeokgung is free every Wednesday, part of the government's expanded **문화가 있는 날** (munhwaga inneun nal), Culture Day. The Secret Garden still needs its own paid ticket.
- **Free entry**: visitors aged 18 and under, those 65 and over, and anyone wearing **[한복](/en/dictionary/?q=한복)** (hanbok) enter the palace grounds free.

## Getting there

Take Line 3 to **안국역** (Angukyeok), Anguk Station, exit 3, and walk along the palace's west wall. The main gate, **돈화문** (Donhwamun), is under repair, so use the side entrance, Geumhomun Gate. Pair your visit with neighbouring **창경궁** (Changgyeonggung), which has its own autumn colours and a glasshouse.

**Tip**: the leaves are at their best on weekday mornings, before the crowds. If the online tickets are gone, go early and queue for the on-site places.
