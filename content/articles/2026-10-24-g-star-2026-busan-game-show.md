Every November, gamers from all over Korea head to Busan for **지스타** (Jiseuta), G-Star, the country's international game **[전시회](/en/dictionary/?q=전시회)** (jeonsihoe), exhibition. For four days, the halls of the BEXCO convention centre fill with demo stations, giant screens, cosplayers, esports matches and long, cheerful queues. In 2026 it runs from **Thursday 19 to Sunday 22 November**. Details below are as of October 2026; organisers can change programmes, so check the official site, gstar.or.kr, before you go.

## What's on in 2026

- **Main halls (B2C)**: open to the public 19–22 November. According to the organisers' September floor plan, all six large zones in BEXCO Exhibition Center 1 are taken, each with more than 100 booths, where Korean and foreign publishers show new titles you can try.
- **Global Game Showcase**: new this year on the first floor of Exhibition Center 2, with Koei Tecmo, Sega, NetEase Games and CD Projekt Red.
- **Indie Showcase 2.0: Galaxy**: 250 booths from 106 independent developers, a good place to talk to the people who made the game.
- **G-CON 2026**: a conference on 19–20 November in BEXCO's grand ballroom, themed around narrative, with talks by game and film storytellers and seats for 1,500.
- **Esports**: the Overwatch Champions Series Asia Stage 3 Grand Final is held in the BEXCO auditorium from 20 to 22 November.
- **Business area (B2B)**: 19–21 November, for industry visitors only.

There is also an online preview show, G-Star Premiere, on 18 November, the day before the doors open.

## Tickets

Entry is by advance booking only, with no regular ticket sales on site. Standard tickets went on sale on the official website on 29 September, at **18,000 won for adults and 8,000 won for youth**. A special pass that adds a *Yumi's Cells* keyring and other extras was sold separately through the shopping app KREAM. Last year, tickets were valid only for the date booked, each person could buy at most two, and you swapped your ticket for a wristband at the entrance, so expect similar rules.

The opening day, 19 November, is also **수능** (Suneung), Korea's university entrance exam day, so students sitting the exam will have to wait for the weekend.

## Getting there

BEXCO is in Haeundae-gu, Busan. Take **Busan Metro Line 2** to **Centum City (BEXCO) Station** and use Exit 1: Exhibition Center 1 is about 50 metres away. Busan Art Museum Station, one stop along, also works (Exit 7 for Exhibition Center 1). From Busan Station, change from Line 1 to Line 2 at Seomyeon.

## Tips

- **Book your day early.** Last year, Saturday's 10:00 entry slot sold out first.
- **Go straight to the game you want.** Demos of big new titles draw the longest queues.
- **Bring a power bank.** You'll use your phone for tickets, maps and photos all day.
- **Make a weekend of it.** Haeundae Beach is a short ride from BEXCO.
