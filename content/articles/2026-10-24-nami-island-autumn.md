**[남이섬](/en/dictionary/?q=남이섬)** (Namiseom), Nami Island, is a small island in the Bukhan River, an easy day trip from Seoul. It became famous abroad as a filming location for the drama *Winter Sonata*, **겨울연가** (gyeoul yeonga), but its real stars are its trees. In late October and early November, the long rows of ginkgo, metasequoia and maple turn the whole island yellow and red. Here's how to get there and what to see. Prices and times are as of October 2026.

## Getting there from Seoul

The island sits in Chuncheon, Gangwon Province, but you reach it from the ferry pier, **[선착장](/en/dictionary/?q=선착장)** (seonchakjang), in Gapyeong, Gyeonggi Province.

- **ITX-Cheongchun train**: fast, reserved-seat trains run to Gapyeong Station. A recent guide lists about 45 minutes and 5,700 won from Cheongnyangni, or about an hour and 6,900 won from Yongsan. Book in the Korail+ app (formerly Korail Talk), as weekend autumn trains fill up.
- **Commuter train**: the slower Gyeongchun Line trains also stop at Gapyeong, and you can pay with a transit card.
- **From Gapyeong Station**: the pier is about 1.5 km away. Take a taxi for a few minutes, a local bus, or walk for around 20 minutes when traffic is jammed.
- **Shuttle bus**: tour-booking sites sell round-trip shuttles from central Seoul. One current timetable picks up at Hongik University Station (9:00), Myeongdong (9:40) and Dongdaemun History & Culture Park (9:50), and leaves the island at 16:30. Book by 10:00 the day before; the ride takes 1.5 to 2.5 hours depending on traffic.

## Ferry, zipline and tickets

The entry ticket includes the round-trip ferry, a crossing of only a few minutes:

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| Adults | 19,000 won |
| Middle and high school students | 16,000 won |
| Children (36 months to primary school) | 13,000 won |

The island is open from 8:00 to 21:00. Ferries leave every 10 to 20 minutes between 9:00 and 18:00, and roughly every 30 minutes outside those hours. For a more dramatic arrival, take the **짚와이어** (jibwaieo [지봐이어]), a zipline from a tall tower near the pier that lands on the island in about a minute. It costs much more than the ferry and includes entry; you return by boat. Check the current price on the official site, namisum.com, before you go.

## What to see

- **Songpa Ginkgo Path** (송파은행나무길): the island's classic autumn sight, a carpet of yellow leaves that peaks in late October. It's the busiest spot, so come early.
- **Metasequoia Path** (메타세쿼이아길): tall, rust-coloured trees in two straight lines, the image on most Nami postcards.
- **The riverside**: walk the edge of the island for quiet views across the water and fewer people.

The island is small enough to walk around at a slow pace in half a day. Wear comfortable shoes, and watch your step after rain, when fallen leaves, **[낙엽](/en/dictionary/?q=낙엽)** (nagyeop), get slippery.

## Tips

- **Go on a weekday.** Weekend roads to Gapyeong get badly jammed in foliage season, and so does parking.
- **Arrive early.** Ginkgo paths look best, and emptiest, in the morning light.
- **Make a day of Gapyeong.** Jarasum, another river island close to the town, is worth a short detour if you have time.
