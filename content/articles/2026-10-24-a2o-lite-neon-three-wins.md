The newest girl group in K-pop has had a very good week. **A2O LITE**, who debuted on October 13, 2026, with the single "NEON," won first place on three music shows in a row: SBS Life's *The Show* on Tuesday, October 20, *Show Champion* on Wednesday, October 21, and *M Countdown* on Thursday, October 22. On *M Countdown* they beat NCT WISH's "I SPY" with 6,866 points.

## A record start on Melon

The trophies follow a fast start on the charts. According to The Korea Herald, "NEON" reached No. 1 on Melon's Top 100 about a day after its release – the fastest for a rookie group's debut song since the chart was revamped in 2021 – and stayed there for six days in a row. It also reached No. 1 on the Bugs real-time chart, No. 3 on Genie and No. 4 on Apple Music Korea.

The first win came just seven days after the debut. On *Show Champion*, "NEON" was up against NCT WISH, PLAVE, FLARE U and Sung Han Bin. The run did stop on Friday: on *Music Bank* on October 23, the trophy went to NCT WISH.

## Who are A2O LITE?

A2O LITE are a girl group from A2O Entertainment, the company founded in 2024 by producer Lee Soo-man, and their debut marks his return to the Korean music industry. The group has nine members from five countries:

- Korea: Young, Vora and Ayune
- Japan: Himeki
- China: Xili, Merci and Aijing
- Thailand: Rada
- Oa, who has US and Japanese citizenship

They are very young: the members are 13 to 17 years old. A2O LITE debuted together with a second, larger group, the 15-member A2O SOUL, at a joint showcase at Kintex in Goyang. Yoo Young-jin, who worked with Lee for years at SM, worked on the music, which the company calls "Zalpha Pop." A2O SOUL's debut album, *Superstar*, is due in November (as of October 2026).

## Why it matters for learners

Music show news is full of short, number-based words. The key one is **1위** (irwi [이뤼]), first place. It is written with the number 1 but read with the Sino-Korean number **일** (il) plus [위](/en/dictionary/?q=위) (wi), rank. Because ㄹ links to the next vowel, 일위 is said [이뤼]. Second place is **2위** (iwi), third **3위** (samwi).

To win, Koreans say **1위를 하다** (irwireul hada) or, in headlines, **1위를 차지하다** (irwireul chajihada), "to take first place." A trophy is **트로피** (teuropi), and the song a winner sings again after the win is the **앙코르** (angkoreu) stage. Fans shorten **음악 방송** (eumak bangsong [으막 방송]), music show, to **음방** (eumbang).

A group in its first year is a [신인](/en/dictionary/?q=신인) (sinin) – a rookie or newcomer – and their first release is their [데뷔](/en/dictionary/?q=데뷔) (debwi), debut.

Use this pattern to talk about any win: **N에서 1위를 했어요** (N-eseo irwireul haesseoyo [이뤼를 해써요]) – "took first place on N." For example, **엠카운트다운에서 1위를 했어요** (emkaunteudauneseo irwireul haesseoyo) – "They won first place on M Countdown."
