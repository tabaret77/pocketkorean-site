## 1. [Politics] Berlin CDU invites Greens and SPD to talk — just before red-red-green negotiations

- **CDU state leader Stefan Evers invited both party leaders** — According to the Tagesspiegel, the confidential meeting is on 29 Oct. The Left Party (Linke) was not invited
- **Greens and SPD said yes, with a caveat** — It is about the transition until a new government is in place, they say, not about forming a coalition. A supplementary budget is one likely topic
- **Background: the 20 Sept election** — Linke 25.7%, CDU 18.8%, AfD 16.3%, Greens 14.3%, SPD 12.1%. The current CDU–SPD coalition lost its majority
- **31 Oct is the turning point** — If a Linke party conference agrees, coalition talks between Linke, Greens and SPD start, and Elif Eralp could become Berlin's first mayor from the Left Party
- **What it means for you** — The plan includes taking flats from big landlords into public ownership, so Berlin renters may want to follow the talks

## 2. [Economy] Supermarket chain Tegut takes down its signs

- **Most of the 288 stores have new owners** — Edeka group 178 (60 of them become Netto), Rewe group 38 (36 Rewe, 2 Penny), the small-store chain Tante Enso 36. The remaining 36 are still looking for a buyer
- **Closures and refits from the end of October** — Each store reopens under its new name on its own date
- **A regional chain founded in Fulda in 1947** — Known for its large organic range. Swiss owner Migros (since 2013) decided to sell in March after heavy losses
- **What it means for you** — If you shop at Tegut in Hesse, Bavaria or Thuringia, use up gift cards and tebonus points soon. It is not yet certain the new operators will accept them

## 3. [Society] After storm Luise comes storm Manja — storm surge on the North Sea

- **Manja arrives from the west on Saturday evening** — Heavy rain in places until Sunday morning, then strong winds almost everywhere except the southeast. Calmer on Monday
- **Gusts of around 100 km/h on the North Sea coast** — High water from Sunday to Monday; in Hamburg the lower part of the St. Pauli fish market may flood
- **Storm Luise injured several people on Friday** — in the west and southwest; fallen trees and damaged overhead lines stopped trains in several places
- **Island ferries affected** — No fast ferries to Helgoland this weekend, and no boats to the Halligen on Sunday and Monday
- **What it means for you** — Cologne and Aachen advise staying out of forests, parks, cemeteries and tree-lined avenues. Check the DB app before any Sunday train trip

## 4. [Culture] Top German literary prize goes to Christine Wunnicke today

- **Ceremony on 24 Oct at the Staatstheater Darmstadt, €50,000** — The Georg Büchner Prize, awarded by the German Academy for Language and Literature, is seen as the most important literary prize in the German language
- **Novelist born in Munich in 1966** — Praised for surprising stories that jump across times and places, told with dry wit
- **Key books** — "Die Dame mit der bemalten Hand" (2020) and "Wachs" (2025) were both shortlisted for the German Book Prize
- **Worth a read?** — Upper-intermediate learners could start with her latest, "Wachs"

## 5. [Arts] Alte Nationalgalerie at 150 — Gursky and Warhol next to 19th-century paintings

- **Open since 22 Oct, until 28 Feb 2027** — The Alte Nationalgalerie on Museum Island opened in 1876 and marks its 150th anniversary with the show "Über Zeiten" (Across the Ages)
- **Modern works placed among the old collection** — On all three floors, 20th- and 21st-century pieces hang next to 19th-century art
- **Names in the title** — Andreas Gursky, Sarah Lucas, Joan Miró, Andy Warhol and others
- **Worth a visit?** — It's built around comparing pairs, not walking through art history, so you don't need any background to enjoy it
