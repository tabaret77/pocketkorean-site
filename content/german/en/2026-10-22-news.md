Hello! Here is the news for Thursday, 22 October.
The clocks change this weekend and the wind picks up, so let's start with what affects your plans.

## 1. This Sunday, the clocks go back one hour

- **Sunday 25 October, at night: 3→2 am** — One extra hour of sleep. Phones switch by themselves; ovens and car clocks don’t.
- **Winter time (CET) starts** — UK & Ireland change the same night
- **Darker evenings** — Sunset feels an hour earlier
- **EU plan to end it is stuck** — No progress since the 2019 vote

| When | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Sun 25 Oct, 03:00 | Back to 02:00 | Reset analogue and kitchen clocks |
| The week after | Darker evenings | Check your bike lights |

## 3. States: cut the electricity tax for everyone

- **Saving for 3 people (4,000 kWh): €93/yr** — Verivox estimate. 2 people: €65, single: €35
- **Now 2.05 ct/kWh** — Goal: EU minimum 0.1 ct
- **Tax revenue +€11bn a year** — New tax estimate on 22 Oct
- **Finance minister hesitant** — “Bring a way to pay for it”

| Household | Use per year | Estimated saving per year |
|---|---|---|
| Single | 1,500 kWh | about €35 |
| 2 people | 2,800 kWh | about €65 |
| 3 people | 4,000 kWh | about €93 |

## 4. Tobacco tax law took effect a year early — by mistake

- **Expected price of a pack in 2027: €8.77** — Finance ministry estimate, 20 cigarettes. Today: about €8
- **In force since 21 October** — Planned start: January 2027
- **Shop prices stay for now** — Customs tolerates old prices
- **About €11.36 by 2030** — Rising step by step

| Year | Expected price of a 20-pack (ministry estimate) |
|---|---|
| Now | about €8 on average |
| 2027 | €8.77 |
| 2030 | €11.36 |

## 5. Regional trains: fewer or pricier?

- **Deutschlandticket per month from Jan 2027: €66.80** — Now €63. This rise is already decided.
- **Track fees jump** — States want Berlin to pay
- **Federal minister says no** — No extra money in his budget
- **Transport ministers met** — 21–22 Oct, Hohenschwangau
