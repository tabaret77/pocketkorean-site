Hello! Here is the news for Thursday, 22 October.
The clocks change this weekend, and the first autumn storm arrives on Friday. One story from each area — politics, economy, society, culture, arts, entertainment and sport.

## 1. [Politics] State premiers: cut the electricity tax for everyone

- **States push the federal government** — Cut the tax from 2.05 ct/kWh to the EU minimum of 0.1 ct
- **A 3-person home (4,000 kWh) would save about €93 a year** — Verivox estimate; 2 people €65, single €35
- **More tax money than expected** — The 22 Oct tax estimate sees about €11bn more a year
- **Finance minister hesitant** — Wants a plan for how to pay for it
- **What it means for you** — Nothing is decided yet; your bill doesn't change for now

## 2. [Economy] Tobacco tax law took effect a year early — by mistake

- **In force since 21 October** — The plan was January 2027; a mistake brought it forward
- **Shop prices stay the same for now** — Customs tolerates the old prices
- **About €8.77 a pack in 2027** — Finance ministry estimate for 20 cigarettes; today about €8
- **€11.36 by 2030** — Prices rise step by step every year

<p class="src">Source: <a href="https://www.zdfheute.de/politik/deutschland/tabak-gesetz-panne-in-kraft-100.html">zdfheute.de</a> · <a href="https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/tabaksteuer-gesetz-aenderung-versehentlich-bundesfinanzministerium">lto.de</a></p>

## 3. [Society] This Sunday, the clocks go back one hour

- **Sunday 25 October: 3 am becomes 2 am** — One extra hour of sleep
- **Phones switch by themselves** — Ovens, microwaves, car clocks and watches don't
- **Winter time (CET) starts** — The UK and Ireland change the same night
- **Darker evenings** — A good week to check your bike lights
- **EU plan to end clock changes is stuck** — No progress since the 2019 Parliament vote

## 4. [Culture] Feminist magazine Emma to close after 50 years

- **Alice Schwarzer (83) announced it on 21 Oct** — She founded the bimonthly magazine in 1977
- **Last issue on 8 December** — A double-length look back at 50 years of topics; one more regular issue comes out on 27 October
- **Not about money, she says** — Sales are falling but still cover costs; the team simply feels it's enough
- **The archive goes to Marbach** — Letters and campaign material will be kept and opened at the German Literature Archive
- **Why it matters** — For decades Emma was the loudest voice of German feminism, and a divisive one

- **A road movie through post-war Germany** — It tackles Wagner's antisemitism and German–Israeli relations today
- **Conductor: Giedrė Šlekytė** — Her first Wagner at this house
- **Want to go?** — Further dates on 28 and 31 Oct and in November, with talks after the later shows
