Missed the last train? Seoul's late-night N buses

2026-10-23

Phrase from this story: 막차 = the last train or busSearch a word

Autumn nights in Seoul are long: Halloween parties, year-end dinners, a late show in Hongdae. Sooner or later you'll look at your watch and realise you've missed the 막차 (makcha), the last subway train. Before you queue for a taxi, check the 심야버스 (simya beoseu), late-night buses. Seoul calls them 올빼미버스 (olppaemi beoseu), "owl buses," and their route numbers all start with N. Details below are as of October 2026.

How the N buses work

According to the Seoul Metropolitan Government's transport page, updated in March 2026:

  • Routes: 14 late-night routes, run by 140 buses in total. They link busy night-time areas in the city centre and sub-centres with residential districts on the edge of the city.
  • Hours: the N-bus network runs between 23:00 and 06:00. Each route has its own first and last bus, and most finish their last runs between about 03:00 and 04:00.
  • Frequency: buses come roughly every 15 to 40 minutes depending on the route, so check the arrival board at the stop instead of just waiting.
  • Fare: 2,500 won by transit card. Use the same T-money or credit card you use on the subway.

The buses are easy to spot: a large N is painted on the outside, and bus stops show the remaining waiting time on their electronic boards. Seoul's 기후동행카드 (gihu donghaeng kadeu), the unlimited monthly Climate Card, also covers N buses.

Some useful routes

Here are five of the 14 routes, with their first and last departures:

Route Between Runs Every
N15 Uidong – Sadang Station 23:30–03:30 15–30 min
N26 Banghwa-dong – Sinnae-dong 00:00–03:25 20–25 min
N30 Gangil-dong – Seoul Station 23:10–03:50 30–35 min
N61 Sinjeong-dong – Sanggye-dong 23:40–04:10 10–30 min
N62 Sinjeong-dong – Myeonmok-dong 23:40–03:10 15–20 min

The route names only give the two ends, so type your destination into a map app before you set off and look for a bus number starting with N.

Tips for a smooth ride

  • Check the last subway first. If you can still catch the 막차, it's usually faster than the bus.
  • Don't wait for the first train. Instead of sitting in a café until the 첫차 (cheotcha), the first train of the morning, an N bus can get you home hours earlier.
  • Press the bell. Push the 하차벨 (hachabel), the stop button, before your stop so the driver knows to pull in.
  • Listen for the stop name. Announcements begin with 이번 정류장은 (ibeon jeongnyujangeun), "this stop is…".

Korean you can use

Korean Romanization Meaning

첫차 cheotcha first train/bus

이 버스 서울역 가요? (i beoseu seoullyeok gayo?) – Does this bus go to Seoul Station? Swap in your stop: 이 버스 사당역 가요? (i beoseu sadangnyeok gayo?).
