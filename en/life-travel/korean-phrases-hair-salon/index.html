Korean for the hair salon: trims, fringes, perms and colour

2026-10-24

Phrase from this story: 다듬어 주세요 = Just a trim, pleaseSearch a word

Korean hair salons are famous for good cuts, careful shampoos and perms that hold their shape. The only hard part for many newcomers is explaining what they want. A photo on your phone helps a lot, but a few phrases will save you from coming out with a much shorter cut than you planned. You'll find a 미용실 (miyongsil), hair salon, on almost every street; men's barbershops are 이발소 (ibalsso [이발쏘]) or, in their trendy form, 바버숍 (babeosyop). Everything here uses the polite -요 (-yo) ending. Prices are as of October 2026.

Booking

Many salons also take bookings online, where you pick a time and a stylist. On the phone or in person:

  1. 커트 예약하고 싶어요. (keoteu yeyakago sipeoyo. [커트 예야카고 시퍼요.]) – I'd like to book a haircut.
  2. 오늘 오후에 자리 있어요? (oneul ohue jari isseoyo?) – Do you have a slot this afternoon?
  3. 디자이너님 지정할 수 있어요? (dijaineonim jijeonghal su isseoyo? [디자이너님 지정할 쑤 이써요?]) – Can I choose a stylist? Stylists are called 디자이너 (dijaineo), "designer." Senior stylists, and the owner, 원장님 (wonjangnim), often charge more.

Describing the cut

The key verb is 다듬다 (dadeumtta [다듬따]), to trim or tidy up.

  1. 조금만 다듬어 주세요. (jogeumman dadeumeo juseyo. [조금만 다드머 주세요.]) – Just a little trim, please.
  2. 짧게 잘라 주세요. (jjalkke jalla juseyo. [짤께 잘라 주세요.]) – Please cut it short.
  3. 이만큼 잘라 주세요. (imankeum jalla juseyo.) – Cut this much off. Show the length with your fingers.
  4. 앞머리 내 주세요. (ammeori nae juseyo. [암머리 내 주세요.]) – I'd like a fringe (bangs). 앞머리 (ammeori) is the fringe; 옆머리 (yeommeori [염머리]) the sides; 뒷머리 (dwinmeori [뒨머리]) the back.
  5. 숱 좀 쳐 주세요. (sut jom chyeo juseyo. [숟 쫌 처 주세요.]) – Please thin it out a bit. 숱 (sut) is the thickness of your hair.
  6. 이 사진처럼 해 주세요. (i sajincheoreom hae juseyo.) – Please make it look like this photo.

Perm and colour

  1. 펌하고 싶어요. (peomhago sipeoyo. [펌하고 시퍼요.]) – I'd like a perm. Popular styles have names, such as 볼륨펌 (bollyumpeom), a volume perm at the roots.
  2. 염색하고 싶어요. (yeomsaekago sipeoyo. [염새카고 시퍼요.]) – I'd like to dye my hair. For regrowth only, ask for 뿌리 염색 (ppuri yeomsaek), root colour.
  3. 좀 더 밝게 해 주세요. (jom deo balkke hae juseyo. [좀 더 발께 해 주세요.]) – A bit lighter, please. The opposite is 어둡게 (eodupkke [어둡께]), darker.

Ask about the price before a perm or colour: long hair often costs extra, a charge called 기장 추가 (gijang chuga).

At the shampoo bowl and the till

Staff will ask you a few questions as they work:

  • 샴푸 해 드릴게요. (syampu hae deurilgeyo.) – I'll wash your hair now.
  • 물 온도 괜찮으세요? (mul ondo gwaenchaneuseyo?) – Is the water temperature OK? Answer 네, 괜찮아요 (ne, gwaenchanayo), or 좀 뜨거워요 (jom tteugeowoyo), "it's a bit hot."
  • 왁스 발라 드릴까요? (wakseu balla deurilkkayo? [왁쓰 발라 드릴까요?]) – Shall I put some wax in?

Then head to the counter and ask 얼마예요? (eolmayeyo?), "How much is it?" According to the Korea Consumer Agency's price survey, a cut cost an average of about 19,600 won for women and 13,900 won for men in November 2025, but prices vary widely with the area and the stylist. Some salons offer a discount if you prepay into a membership, 회원권 (hoewongwon); don't feel pressured to sign up on your first visit.

[part of hair] + 만 조금 다듬어 주세요. – 앞머리만 조금 다듬어 주세요. (ammeoriman jogeum dadeumeo juseyo. [암머리만 조금 다드머 주세요.]) – Just a little trim on the fringe, please. -만 (-man) means "only," so the stylist knows to leave the rest alone.
