Korean fried chicken and chimaek: how to order

2026-10-23

Startandstar / CC0 (Wikimedia Commons)

Phrase from this story: 치맥 = chicken and beerSearch a word

Ask Koreans what to eat on a cool autumn evening with friends, and many will say 치킨 (chikin): crunchy fried chicken, often delivered to your door. Eat it with a cold beer and you have 치맥 (chimaek), short for chikin + maekju, beer. It's one of the easiest Korean meals to order, as long as you know a few words. Prices below are as of October 2026.

A short history

Korean fried chicken has grown up fast. Lims Chicken, which opened in 1977, is usually called the first fried chicken shop. In 1982 the Daejeon brand Pelicana created the first chicken coated in a sweet and spicy sauce, the ancestor of today's 양념 (yangnyeom) chicken. After the 1997 financial crisis, many laid-off workers opened chicken franchises, and during the 2002 World Cup, hosted by Korea and Japan, chicken and beer became the classic match-watching meal. By the early 2010s Korea had around 31,000 fried chicken shops.

The menu, decoded

Almost every chicken shop uses the same basic words:

  • 후라이드 (huraideu) – plain fried chicken, crisp and lightly seasoned.
  • 양념 (yangnyeom) – fried chicken tossed in a sticky, sweet-spicy red sauce.
  • 간장 (ganjang) – soy-garlic glaze.
  • 반반 (banban) – half and half, usually half fried and half yangnyeom. The best choice if you can't decide.
  • 순살 (sunsal) – boneless. The opposite, with bones, is 뼈 (ppyeo).

Chicken is counted in whole birds: 한 마리 (han mari), one chicken, enough for two people with a side. Every order comes with 치킨무 (chikinmu), cubes of sweet-and-sour pickled radish that cut through the oil. Many shops add a small cabbage salad too.

Prices and ordering

As of 2026, chicken is no longer cheap. SBS Biz reported in March 2026 that new menu items at popular franchises cost in the mid-to-high 20,000-won range for one chicken. Add a delivery fee of about 3,000–4,000 won and a delivered chicken can pass 30,000 won before you even order a drink. Rising prices for raw chicken were one reason.

Most Koreans order through delivery apps, but eating in at a 호프집 (hopeujip), a casual beer pub, is the real chimaek experience: chicken in a basket, draft beer in big glasses and a noisy table of friends. If you are ordering at the counter or by phone, all you need is one sentence: the flavour, then the number.

Tip: sauced chicken goes soft quickly. If you're taking it home or ordering delivery, plain fried keeps its crunch better.

Korean you can use

Korean Romanization Meaning
치킨 chikin fried chicken
치맥 chimaek chicken and beer
맥주 maekjju [맥쭈] beer
양념 yangnyeom seasoning; sauced chicken
반반 banban half and half
순살 sunsal boneless
배달 baedal delivery
마리 mari counter for animals

반반으로 한 마리 주세요. (banbaneuro han mari juseyo.) – One chicken, half and half, please. Swap 반반 for 후라이드 or 양념, and the number for 두 마리 (du mari), two chickens.
