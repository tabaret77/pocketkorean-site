NCT WISH sell 1.2 million copies of "I SPY" on day one

2026-10-23

Phrase from this story: 초동 = first-week album salesSearch a word
Official pageSM Entertainment – NCT WISH "I SPY" MVPoster, trailer and episode info↗

NCT WISH have another million-seller. The SM Entertainment group's fourth mini album, I SPY, sold 1,559,051 copies in its first week, according to the Hanteo Chart figures announced on October 19, 2026. Most of that came in a single day: 1,200,086 copies on release day alone. The album also topped both Hanteo's World Chart and Album Chart for the third week of October.

The album

I SPY came out on Monday, October 12, at 6 p.m. KST. It has six songs: the title track "I SPY," plus "We Own This Street," "UPGRADE," "Super Major," "Hubble Time" and "Like A Dream." SM describes the title song as hybrid dance-pop about the moment you fall for someone – the curiosity, the excitement and the wish to pull that person into your world.

NCT WISH debuted in 2024 as a six-member NCT unit with Korean and Japanese members. Next up is a big stage: two concerts at Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul on January 15 and 16, 2027, part of their second tour, INTO THE WISH: Face Our WISH (as of October 2026).

Up or down?

The headline number is huge, but Sports Kyunghyang pointed out that it is lower than last time. The group's first full album, Ode to Love (April 2026), sold 1,825,925 copies in its first week, so I SPY is about 267,000 copies, or 14.6%, behind. Most of the gap came after day one: on days two to seven, I SPY sold 358,965 copies, compared with 540,741 for Ode to Love.

The paper also noted that the comparison is not quite fair, because a full album and a mini album are different products. Over a longer view, the group keeps growing: their second mini album, poppop (April 2025), sold 1,087,838 in its first week, and the third, COLOR (September 2025), sold 1,395,217. I SPY is their fourth million-selling album in a row.

Why it matters for learners

K-pop fans talk about sales with a handful of short words built from Sino-Korean roots. The most important is 초동 (chodong), short for 초동 판매량 (chodong panmaeryang), first-week sales – literally "beginning" (초) + "movement" (동). Fans compare 초동 numbers the way drama fans compare ratings.

Two more you will see in K-pop news:

  • 첫날 (cheonnal) – the first day. Note the sound change: 첫날 is written cheot-nal but said [천날].
  • 2관왕 (igwanwang) – a "double crown," topping two charts or winning two awards. The 관 means crown.

To talk about the type of release, use 미니 앨범 (mini aelbeom) for a mini album and 정규 앨범 (jeonggyu aelbeom) for a full-length album. A physical album is also called an 음반 (eumban), and a record is a 기록 (girok).

A pattern for comparing numbers: 지난 앨범보다 적게 팔렸어요 (jinan aelbeomboda jeokke pallyeosseoyo [지난 앨범보다 적께 팔려써요]) – "It sold fewer than the last album." Noun + 보다 means "than"; swap 적게 (fewer) for 많이 (more) to say the opposite: 지난 앨범보다 많이 팔렸어요.
