-(으)ㄹ진대

since, given that … (archaic; leads to a conclusion)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Archaic, used in formal writing or speeches. Clause 1 states an accepted premise; clause 2 draws a conclusion from it, very often a rhetorical question with 어찌 or 하물며.

Forming it

하다→ 할진대
없다→ 없을진대
학생→ 학생일진대
부모→ 부모일진대

Not for everyday speech or practical situations: ✗ 비가 올진대 우산 가져가.

Examples

  • 부모도 자식을 이기지 못할진대 하물며 남이 어찌 이기겠는가.

    If even parents cannot win against their children, how much less could a stranger?

  • 사정이 이러할진대 누구를 탓할 수 있겠습니까?

    Since this is how things stand, whom could we blame?

Compared with similar patterns

-거늘 is also archaic but often adds reproach or contrast ('and yet'); -(으)니 is the neutral written 'since/so'.
