N은/는 물론(이고)

not to mention N, N of course (and also …)

Level 4, TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Says that N obviously applies, and something else does too. Clause 2 usually has 도 or 까지.

Forming it

영어→ 영어는 물론
한국→ 한국은 물론이고
맛있다→ 맛있는 것은 물론이고

Examples

  • 그 가수는 한국은 물론 외국에서도 인기가 많아요.

    That singer is popular not only in Korea but abroad as well.

  • 이 식당은 음식이 맛있는 것은 물론이고 값도 싸요.

    The food here is delicious, of course, and it's cheap too.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 (G3-024) means 'not only … but also' without the 'obviously' nuance. -기도 하다 (G2-053) simply adds 'also'.
