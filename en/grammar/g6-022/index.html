-(으)리라

will surely …; I shall … (literary)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

A literary future: the speaker's firm will (first person) or a confident prediction (third person). In modern Korean it is common before 생각하다, 믿다, 다짐하다, 예상하다, often as -(으)리라고.

Forming it

하다→ 하리라
오다→ 오리라
믿다→ 믿으리라
사실→ 사실이리라

Examples

  • 다시는 같은 실수를 하지 않으리라 다짐했다.

    I resolved never to make the same mistake again.

  • 그가 반드시 돌아오리라고 굳게 믿습니다.

    I firmly believe that he will surely come back.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 거예요 and -겠- are the everyday future/conjecture; -노라 declares or announces, while -(으)리라 resolves or predicts.
