-지 못하다

cannot, could not

Level 1, TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Means 'cannot / could not': the subject would like to, but can't because of ability or circumstances. The short form 못 + V is more common in speech.

Forming it

가다→ 가지 못해요 / 못 가요
먹다→ 먹지 못해요
수영하다→ 수영 못 해요

With noun + 하다 verbs, 못 goes before 하다: 운동 못 해요 (✗ 못 운동해요). Normally verbs only; for adjectives use -지 않다. If it's your choice, use 안 instead.

Examples

  • 어제는 바빠서 운동을 하지 못했어요.

    I was busy yesterday, so I couldn't exercise.

  • 저는 매운 음식을 못 먹어요.

    I can't eat spicy food.

Compared with similar patterns

안/-지 않다 = don't (choice or simple fact). -(으)ㄹ 수 없다 = cannot; very close in meaning and often interchangeable.

Related patterns

  • -지 않다 do not, is not (negation of verbs and adjectives)
  • -(으)ㄹ 수 있다/없다 can / cannot; be possible / impossible
