-다(가) 말다

stop (doing) halfway, start … but not finish

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

An action is started and then stopped before it's finished. Common forms: -다(가) 말았어요, -다(가) 말고 (stopping one thing to do another), -다(가) 만 N (a half-done thing: 먹다 만 빵).

Forming it

먹다→ 먹다가 말았어요
하다→ 하다 말고
읽다→ 읽다 만 책

Examples

  • 친구는 영화를 보다가 말고 갑자기 나가 버렸어요.

    My friend stopped watching the movie halfway and suddenly walked out.

  • 숙제를 하다 말고 어디 가요?

    Where are you going in the middle of your homework?

Compared with similar patterns

-다가 (G2-035) simply switches to another action; -다(가) 말다 focuses on leaving the first one unfinished.
