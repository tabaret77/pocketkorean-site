-는 동안

while, during (a span of time); N 동안 = for/during N

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Marks the period of time during which something else happens; the two clauses can have different subjects. With time nouns: 3년 동안 'for three years', 방학 동안 'during the vacation'.

Forming it

자다→ 자는 동안
읽다→ 읽는 동안
살다→ 사는 동안
방학→ 방학 동안

Use -는 even for past events: ✗ 제가 없은 동안 → 제가 없는 동안. 동안 is a duration; for a point in time use -(으)ㄹ 때.

Examples

  • 제가 요리하는 동안 남편은 청소를 했어요.

    While I cooked, my husband cleaned.

  • 방학 동안 고향에 다녀왔어요.

    I visited my hometown during the vacation.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)면서 needs one subject doing two things at once; -는 사이(에) stresses a short gap in which something (often unexpected) happens; -는 도중(에) means in the middle of an action or trip.
