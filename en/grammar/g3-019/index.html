-도록 하다

make sure to …; (I) will see to it; have someone do

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

With -(으)세요 it is a polite instruction ('please make sure to'); with -겠습니다 it is a firm promise by the speaker. In 'A가 B에게 V-도록 하다' it means A makes or has B do something.

Forming it

오다→ 오도록 하세요
확인하다→ 확인하도록 하겠습니다
늦다→ 늦지 않도록 하세요

The instruction/promise use is formal; it sounds stiff between close friends.

Examples

  • 내일은 회의에 늦지 않도록 하세요.

    Make sure you're not late for the meeting tomorrow.

  • 다음부터는 더 조심하도록 하겠습니다.

    I'll be more careful from now on.

Compared with similar patterns

-게 하다 is the common causative; -도록 하다 also works as a causative but is mostly used for polite instructions and resolutions. -도록 alone is a connective (so that).
