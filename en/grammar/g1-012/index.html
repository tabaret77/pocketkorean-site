-(으)시-

subject honorific (shows respect to the subject)

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Shows respect to the SUBJECT of the sentence (older people, superiors, customers). It goes right after the stem and before tense and endings: 가+시+었+어요 → 가셨어요, 가+시+ㅂ니다 → 가십니다.

Forming it

가다→ 가셨어요
읽다→ 읽으세요
바쁘다→ 바쁘세요
선생님→ 선생님이세요

Never use it about yourself: ✗ 제가 가세요. Some verbs have special forms: 드시다 (먹다), 주무시다 (자다), 계시다 (있다, be somewhere), 말씀하시다 (말하다). The subject particle can become 께서.

Examples

  • 우리 아버지는 은행에서 일하세요.

    My father works at a bank.

  • 사장님께서 방금 회의실로 가셨어요.

    The company president just went to the meeting room.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)세요 is -(으)시- + -어요; it can be an honorific statement or a polite request depending on context.
