-는 것

nominalizer: turns a verb into a noun (doing, the act of -ing)

Level 2, TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Turns a verb phrase into a noun so it can be a subject or object: 노래하는 것을 좋아해요 'I like singing'. In speech 것이 → 게, 것을 → 걸, 것은 → 건.

Forming it

보다→ 보는 것
먹다→ 먹는 것
만들다→ 만드는 것
듣다→ 듣는 것

Use -는 for present/general actions; for a past action use -(으)ㄴ 것 (어제 먹은 것). Don't drop 것: ✗ 음악을 듣는 좋아해요.

Examples

  • 저는 음악을 듣는 것을 좋아해요.

    I like listening to music.

  • 외국어를 배우는 게 생각보다 어려워요.

    Learning a foreign language is harder than I thought.

Compared with similar patterns

-기 is shorter and mostly used in fixed patterns, lists and headings; -는 것 is the general, flexible nominalizer.

Related patterns

  • -기 nominalizer -ing (in set phrases, hobbies, skills, lists)
  • -기(가) 쉽다/어렵다 easy / hard to do (also: convenient, good to do)
