N(이)라도

at least N, N or something (not ideal, but acceptable)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Shows that N is not the best choice but is acceptable as a second option. Usually with suggestions, requests or wishes. With question words it means 'any…': 누구라도, 언제라도.

Forming it

물→ 물이라도
커피→ 커피라도
라면→ 라면이라도
전화→ 전화라도

Don't use it for plain facts in the past: ✗ 어제 라면이라도 먹었어요 (to mean 'I ate ramen').

Examples

  • 밥이 없으면 라면이라도 먹을까요?

    If there's no rice, shall we at least eat ramen?

  • 바빠서 못 오면 전화라도 해 주세요.

    If you're too busy to come, at least give me a call.
