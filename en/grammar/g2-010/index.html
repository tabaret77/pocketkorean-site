-고 나서

after (finishing) doing

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

The first action is completely finished, then the second follows. It stresses completion more than plain -고. Only with verbs.

Forming it

먹다→ 먹고 나서
끝나다→ 끝나고 나서
씻다→ 씻고 나서

No past tense before it: ✗ 먹었고 나서 → 먹고 나서. Not used with adjectives or 이다.

Examples

  • 숙제를 다 하고 나서 게임을 해요.

    After I finish all my homework, I play games.

  • 영화가 끝나고 나서 저녁을 먹었어요.

    After the movie ended, we had dinner.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄴ 후에 is similar and also works with time nouns (식사 후에); -고 only lists actions in order; -자마자 means immediately after.
