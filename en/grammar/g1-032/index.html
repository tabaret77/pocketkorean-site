-(으)려고 하다

intend to, plan to; be about to

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

1) An intention or plan: 'intend to, plan to'. 2) Something about to happen: 'be about to'. The past -(으)려고 했어요 often means 'I meant to (but didn't)'.

Forming it

가다→ 가려고 해요
먹다→ 먹으려고 해요
만들다→ 만들려고 해요
듣다→ 들으려고 해요

Verbs only. ㄹ-stems take -려고: 만들다 → 만들려고 (✗ 만들으려고).

Examples

  • 이번 주말에 집을 청소하려고 해요.

    I'm planning to clean the house this weekend.

  • 기차가 곧 출발하려고 해요.

    The train is about to leave.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 거예요 is a plainer future or plan. -(으)려고 without 하다 means 'in order to'. -(으)ㄹ까 하다 is a vaguer 'thinking of doing'.
