-(으)나

but, however (written)

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Contrasts two clauses like -지만, but is used mainly in writing, news and formal speeches.

Forming it

가다→ 가나
좋다→ 좋으나
만들다→ 만드나
했다→ 했으나

Do not confuse with N(이)나 'or / about'. In casual speech use -지만 instead.

Examples

  • 열심히 노력했으나 좋은 결과를 얻지 못했다.

    I worked hard, but I did not get a good result.

  • 이 제품은 가격이 비싸나 품질이 좋다.

    This product is expensive, but its quality is good.

Compared with similar patterns

-지만 is the everyday form for 'but'. -건만 (T6) is literary and adds disappointment that an expectation was not met.
