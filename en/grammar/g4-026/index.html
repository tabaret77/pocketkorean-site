-길래

since, because (I saw / heard that …) — reason for my action

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Spoken. Clause 1 is a situation the speaker noticed or someone else's action; clause 2 is what the speaker did because of it, usually in the past. With a question word it asks the reason: 무슨 일이길래 그렇게 울어요? 'What happened that you're crying like that?'

Forming it

오다→ 오길래
싸다→ 싸길래
맛있다→ 맛있길래
주말→ 주말이길래

Clause 2 can't be a command, suggestion or future plan: ✗ 비가 오길래 우산을 가져가세요. The subject of clause 2 is usually the speaker.

Examples

  • 과일이 싸고 맛있길래 많이 샀어요.

    The fruit was cheap and tasty, so I bought a lot.

  • 밖에 비가 오길래 우산을 가지고 나왔어요.

    It was raining outside, so I brought an umbrella.

Compared with similar patterns

-기에 (G5-001) is the written equivalent. -아/어서 (G1-019) and -(으)니까 (G1-027) give a reason without the 'I noticed' nuance; -(으)니까 also allows commands.
