N이에요/예요

is N, am N, are N (polite)

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Attaches to a noun to say what someone or something is. Use 이에요 after a final consonant and 예요 after a vowel. The same form is a question with rising intonation (학생이에요?).

Forming it

학생→ 학생이에요
의사→ 의사예요
가방→ 가방이에요
친구→ 친구예요

Pick the form by the last sound: ✗ 학생예요, ✗ 의사이에요. ✗ 이예요 is a common spelling mistake.

Examples

  • 저는 한국어 선생님이에요.

    I am a Korean teacher.

  • 이 사람은 우리 반 친구예요.

    This person is a friend from my class.

Compared with similar patterns

N이/가 아니에요 is the negative ('is not N'). N입니다 is the formal -ㅂ/습니다 version used in speeches and presentations.
