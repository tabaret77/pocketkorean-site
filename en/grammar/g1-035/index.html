-(으)면 안 되다

must not, not allowed to

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Expresses prohibition: 'must not, not allowed to'. Used for rules and for refusing permission (아니요, 들어가면 안 돼요).

Forming it

가다→ 가면 안 돼요
찍다→ 찍으면 안 돼요
만들다→ 만들면 안 돼요
듣다→ 들으면 안 돼요

Examples

  • 박물관 안에서 사진을 찍으면 안 돼요.

    You can't take pictures inside the museum.

  • 감기에 걸렸으니까 찬물을 마시면 안 돼요.

    You have a cold, so you shouldn't drink cold water.

Compared with similar patterns

-지 마세요 is a direct request ('please don't'); -(으)면 안 되다 states the rule. -아/어도 되다 is the opposite (permission). -지 않으면 안 되다 is a double negative meaning 'must'.
