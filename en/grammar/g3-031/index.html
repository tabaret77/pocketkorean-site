-느라(고)

because (I was busy) doing …

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Clause 1 is an ongoing action that took time or effort and caused a (usually negative) result in clause 2: couldn't do something, was tired, etc. Same subject in both clauses; verbs only.

Forming it

공부하다→ 공부하느라고
자다→ 자느라
놀다→ 노느라
찾다→ 찾느라고

No tense marker before it and no command/suggestion after it: ✗ 공부하느라고 일찍 자세요. Not for adjectives or events nobody controls: ✗ 비가 오느라고.

Examples

  • 어제 시험공부를 하느라고 한숨도 못 잤어요.

    I couldn't sleep a wink last night because I was studying for an exam.

  • 게임하느라 시간 가는 줄 몰랐어요.

    I was playing a game and lost track of time.

Compared with similar patterns

-아/어서 is a general reason. -는 바람에 is an unexpected event and can have a different subject.
