-(으)면 좋겠다

I wish, I hope (that)

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Expresses a wish or hope about yourself, others or a situation. The form -았/었으면 좋겠다 is very common and makes the wish sound stronger.

Forming it

오다→ 오면 좋겠어요
받다→ 받으면 좋겠어요
듣다→ 들으면 좋겠어요
만나다→ 만났으면 좋겠어요

The -았/었- here is not past: 내일 비가 안 왔으면 좋겠어요 = I hope it won't rain tomorrow.

Examples

  • 내일은 비가 안 왔으면 좋겠어요.

    I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow.

  • 빨리 방학이 됐으면 좋겠어요.

    I wish vacation would come soon.

Compared with similar patterns

-기 바라다 is a more formal wish, used in writing and greetings; -고 싶다 is your own desire to do something; -는 게 좋겠다 is a suggestion.
