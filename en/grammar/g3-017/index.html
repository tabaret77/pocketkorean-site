Korean grammar patterns › Level 3

-(으)ㄹ 만하다

be worth doing; be OK / doable

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Says something is worth doing (a recommendation) or is doable/acceptable — not great, but fine.

Forming it

보다→ 볼 만하다
먹다→ 먹을 만하다
살다→ 살 만하다
듣다→ 들을 만하다

Attaches to verbs only: ✗ 예쁠 만하다. ㄷ irregular: 듣다 → 들을 만하다.

Examples

  • 그 영화는 시간 내서 한번 볼 만해요.

    That movie is worth making time to see.

  • 처음에는 힘들었는데 이제 일이 할 만해요.

    It was hard at first, but now the work is manageable.
