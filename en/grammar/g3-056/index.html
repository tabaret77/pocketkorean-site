-(으)ㄹ 리가 없다

there's no way that …; it can't be that …

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Expresses strong certainty that something is impossible. The question -(으)ㄹ 리가 있어요? is rhetorical (= no way).

Forming it

오다→ 올 리가 없어요
있다→ 있을 리가 없어요
거짓말→ 거짓말일 리가 없어요
알다→ 알 리가 없어요

Examples

  • 그 친구가 거짓말을 할 리가 없어요.

    There's no way that friend would lie.

  • 어제 산 우유가 벌써 상했을 리가 없어요.

    There's no way the milk I bought yesterday has already gone bad.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ지도 모르다 leaves room for possibility; -(으)ㄹ 게 뻔하다 says something is obviously true or will surely happen.
