-던

that (someone) used to do / was doing (unfinished)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Noun modifier that recalls a past action that was repeated over time or was interrupted (not finished), or a past state. 자주 가던 식당 = the restaurant I used to go to; 먹던 빵 = bread I was eating (some is left).

Forming it

다니다→ 다니던
먹다→ 먹던
예쁘다→ 예쁘던
학생→ 학생이던

먹은 빵 means the eating is finished; 먹던 빵 means it was not finished or was habitual.

Examples

  • 이건 제가 어릴 때 자주 듣던 노래예요.

    This is a song I often listened to when I was little.

  • 아까 마시던 커피 어디에 뒀어요?

    Where did you put the coffee you were drinking earlier?

Compared with similar patterns

-았/었던 stresses that the past action is completed or the state no longer holds, and can be a one-time event. -(으)ㄴ is plain past completion.
