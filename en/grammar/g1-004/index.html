-아/어요

polite everyday ending (present)

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

The standard polite ending for daily conversation with strangers, coworkers and most adults. The same form can be a statement, a question (rising intonation), a request or a suggestion, depending on context.

Forming it

가다→ 가요
먹다→ 먹어요
공부하다→ 공부해요
듣다→ 들어요

Vowels contract: 가+아요 → 가요, 오+아요 → 와요, 마시+어요 → 마셔요, 배우+어요 → 배워요. Watch irregular stems: 듣다 → 들어요, 덥다 → 더워요, 쓰다 → 써요.

Examples

  • 저는 매일 아침에 커피를 마셔요.

    I drink coffee every morning.

  • 이 식당 음식이 정말 맛있어요.

    The food at this restaurant is really delicious.

Compared with similar patterns

-ㅂ/습니다 is more formal and is used in news, presentations, interviews and announcements; -아/어요 is softer and used in everyday talk.
