-(으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 만큼

as much as, to the degree that; since (given that it is so)

Level 4, TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Shows an amount or degree equal to clause 1: 먹을 만큼 'as much as you'll eat', 노력한 만큼 'as much as you worked'. With A-(으)ㄴ / V-는 / N인 만큼 it can also give a reason ('since, as'), mostly in formal speech: 중요한 시험인 만큼 준비를 잘하세요.

Forming it

먹다→ 먹을 만큼
노력하다→ 노력한 만큼
원하다→ 원하는 만큼
언니→ 언니만큼

Adjectives take -(으)ㄴ, not -는: ✗ 필요하는 만큼 → 필요한 만큼. After a noun, 만큼 is a particle written together: 언니만큼.

Examples

  • 음식은 먹을 만큼만 접시에 담으세요.

    Only put as much food on your plate as you'll eat.

  • 열심히 노력한 만큼 좋은 결과가 있을 거예요.

    You'll get good results in proportion to how hard you've worked.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 정도로 (G3-054) stresses an extreme degree ('so … that'); 만큼 stresses an equal amount or proportion. -(으)니까 (G1-027) is the everyday 'because'; reason-만큼 is more formal and means 'given how much …'.
