-(으)ㄹ 나위(가) 없다

needless to …; couldn't be better

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Means there is no room or need for the action. Used mainly in fixed phrases: 더할 나위 없다 'perfect', 말할 나위(도) 없다 'needless to say', 바랄 나위 없다 'nothing more to wish for'.

Forming it

더하다→ 더할 나위 없다
말하다→ 말할 나위가 없다
바라다→ 바랄 나위 없다

Works with only a few verbs; don't create new combinations freely.

Examples

  • 오늘 날씨는 더할 나위 없이 좋아요.

    Today's weather couldn't be better.

  • 그 사람의 실력은 말할 나위도 없이 뛰어나요.

    Needless to say, that person's skills are excellent.
