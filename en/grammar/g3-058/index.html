-(으)ㄴ/는 데다(가)

on top of, in addition to (…, and also)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Adds another fact in the same direction to clause 1; clause 2 often has 도 or 까지. Past: -(으)ㄴ 데다가 (verbs).

Forming it

싸다→ 싼 데다가
오다→ 오는 데다가
맛있다→ 맛있는 데다가
학생→ 학생인 데다가

Both facts should point the same way: ✗ 값이 싼 데다가 맛이 없어요.

Examples

  • 이 식당은 값이 싼 데다가 맛도 좋아요.

    This restaurant is cheap, and on top of that the food is good.

  • 비가 오는 데다가 바람까지 불어요.

    It's raining, and on top of that the wind is blowing.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 is a bit more formal; -고 해서 gives one reason among several.
