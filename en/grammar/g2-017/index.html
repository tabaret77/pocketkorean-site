-게 되다

come to (do), end up (doing), it turned out that

Level 2, TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Shows that a situation changed or something happened because of circumstances, not simply your own decision. Usually past (-게 됐어요). Also used to announce news modestly: 이번에 결혼하게 됐어요.

Forming it

가다→ 가게 됐어요
알다→ 알게 됐어요
좋아하다→ 좋아하게 됐어요

The tense goes on 되다: ✗ 갔게 됐어요 → 가게 됐어요.

Examples

  • 회사 일 때문에 부산으로 이사하게 됐어요.

    I ended up moving to Busan because of work.

  • 처음엔 김치를 못 먹었는데 지금은 좋아하게 됐어요.

    At first I couldn't eat kimchi, but now I've come to like it.

Compared with similar patterns

N이/가 되다 = become N; -아/어지다 is a change of state, mainly with adjectives (예뻐지다); -기로 하다 is your own active decision.

Related patterns
