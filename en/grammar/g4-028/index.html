-(으)ㄹ걸 그랬다

I should have … (regret); -지 말걸: I shouldn't have …

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

The speaker regrets not having done (or having done) something. Spoken, mostly about oneself. Often shortened to -(으)ㄹ걸 at the end of a sentence in casual speech.

Forming it

오다→ 올걸 그랬어요
먹다→ 먹을걸 그랬어요
듣다→ 들을걸 그랬어요
사다→ 사지 말걸 그랬어요

No past tense inside: ✗ 탔을걸 그랬어요 → 탈걸 그랬어요.

Examples

  • 비가 오네요. 우산을 가지고 올걸 그랬어요.

    It's raining. I should have brought an umbrella.

  • 이렇게 맛있는 줄 알았으면 더 많이 살걸.

    If I'd known it was this good, I would have bought more.

Compared with similar patterns

-았/었어야 했다 (G4-029) is stronger and also used to criticize others; -(으)ㄹ걸 그랬다 is a softer personal regret. -(으)ㄹ걸요 (G3-038) looks similar but is a guess ('probably'), with no regret.
