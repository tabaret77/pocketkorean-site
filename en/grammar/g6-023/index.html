-(으)ㄹ쏘냐

how could … ? (of course not!) (archaic, rhetorical)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

An old-style rhetorical question that strongly denies something, often in a defiant tone: 'Would I …? Never!' Found in old texts, sayings, and humorous or dramatic speech.

Forming it

지다→ 질쏘냐
포기하다→ 포기할쏘냐
두렵다→ 두려울쏘냐
남→ 남일쏘냐

The implied answer is always 'no'; it is not a real question.

Examples

  • 여기까지 왔는데 이제 와서 포기할쏘냐.

    I've come this far; would I give up now? Never.

  • 그깟 추위가 두려울쏘냐? 계속 가자.

    Would I be afraid of such a little cold? Let's keep going.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 리가 없다 is a plain statement that something can't be so; -(으)ㄹ쏘냐 says the same through a defiant rhetorical question.
