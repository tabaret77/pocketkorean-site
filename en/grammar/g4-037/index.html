-(으)ㄹ까 싶다

I'm thinking of …; (worried) that … might

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

With the speaker's own action it's a tentative plan: 'I'm thinking I might …'. -(으)ㄹ까 싶어서 often expresses worry ('in case …, for fear that …'), followed by the precaution taken.

Forming it

가다→ 갈까 싶어요
먹다→ 먹을까 싶어요
막히다→ 막힐까 싶어서
만들다→ 만들까 싶어요

As a plan, it's only for the speaker and not very firm.

Examples

  • 주말에 오랜만에 고향에 갈까 싶어요.

    I'm thinking of going back to my hometown this weekend for the first time in a while.

  • 길이 막힐까 싶어서 일찍 나왔어요.

    I left early in case the roads were busy.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ까 하다 (G2-011) is the same tentative plan. -(으)ㄹ까 봐(서) (G3-013) is the usual way to say 'for fear that'; -(으)ㄹ까 싶어서 is similar but softer.
