-기 일쑤이다

often (do something bad), habitually

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Describes something undesirable that happens frequently as a habit, based on real experience.

Forming it

늦다→ 늦기 일쑤이다
거르다→ 거르기 일쑤예요
잊다→ 잊기 일쑤였어요

Not for one-time events or predictions, and not for good habits: ✗ 매일 운동하기 일쑤예요.

Examples

  • 그 친구는 약속 시간에 늦기 일쑤예요.

    That friend is often late for appointments.

  • 바쁘다 보니 아침을 거르기 일쑤예요.

    I'm so busy that I often skip breakfast.

Compared with similar patterns

-기 십상이다 predicts a likely bad result; -기 일쑤이다 reports a bad thing that repeatedly happens.
