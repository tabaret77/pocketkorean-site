N을/를 비롯해(서)

including, starting with N (and others)

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Names N as the main or first example of a larger group. Common in news and formal speech. Before a noun use N을 비롯한 N.

Forming it

서울→ 서울을 비롯해서
부모님→ 부모님을 비롯한
김치→ 김치를 비롯해

Examples

  • 서울을 비롯해서 전국에 비가 내리겠습니다.

    It will rain across the country, including Seoul.

  • 사장님을 비롯한 모든 직원이 회의에 참석했습니다.

    All the employees, including the president, attended the meeting.

Compared with similar patterns

N은/는 물론 means 'not to mention N' (N is obvious); N을 비롯해 presents N as the leading item of a list.
