-(으)므로

because, since (formal / written)

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Gives a reason or logical ground in formal writing, notices, reports and speeches.

Forming it

오다→ 오므로
많다→ 많으므로
약하다→ 약하므로
학생→ 학생이므로

Too stiff for everyday conversation. Generally not followed by commands or suggestions; use -(으)니까 there.

Examples

  • 이 지역은 비가 많이 오므로 홍수에 주의해야 한다.

    This area gets a lot of rain, so you must watch out for floods.

  • 이 제품은 열에 약하므로 서늘한 곳에 보관해야 합니다.

    This product is sensitive to heat, so it must be stored in a cool place.

Compared with similar patterns

-기 때문에 (G2-007) works in both speech and writing. -기에 (G5-001) is also written but more literary. -(으)니까 (G1-027) is conversational and allows commands.
