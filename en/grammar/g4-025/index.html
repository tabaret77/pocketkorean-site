-(으)며

and; while (written equivalent of -고 / -(으)면서)

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

In writing and formal speech, it lists facts like -고 ('and') or shows two actions happening at the same time like -(으)면서 ('while').

Forming it

듣다→ 들으며
웃다→ 웃으며
편리하다→ 편리하며
학생→ 학생이며

Examples

  • 그는 음악을 들으며 책을 읽었다.

    He read a book while listening to music.

  • 이 아파트는 교통이 편리하며 주변 환경도 깨끗하다.

    This apartment has convenient transportation, and the surroundings are clean too.

Compared with similar patterns

-고 (G1-009) and -(으)면서 (G2-008) are the everyday spoken forms; -(으)며 sounds written or formal.
