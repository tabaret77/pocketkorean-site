-자마자

as soon as

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Clause 2 happens right after clause 1. Tense goes at the end of the sentence; it can be followed by commands and suggestions.

Forming it

도착하다→ 도착하자마자
먹다→ 먹자마자
듣다→ 듣자마자
끝나다→ 끝나자마자

No -았/었- before it: ✗ 끝났자마자.

Examples

  • 집에 도착하자마자 샤워를 했어요.

    As soon as I got home, I took a shower.

  • 수업이 끝나자마자 전화할게요.

    I'll call you as soon as class is over.

Compared with similar patterns

-는 대로 (as soon as) is for future plans and requests, not past events. -자 is written style; -기가 무섭게 is more vivid and idiomatic.
