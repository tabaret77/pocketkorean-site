-고 보니(까)

after doing …, (I realized / found that) …

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

After completing an action, the speaker realized something they hadn't known before. Clause 2 is usually past, often with -더라고요 or -았/었네요.

Forming it

듣다→ 듣고 보니
사다→ 사고 보니까
오다→ 오고 보니

Clause 1 is a completed action without a tense marker (✗ 들었고 보니).

Examples

  • 설명을 듣고 보니 이해가 됐어요.

    Once I heard the explanation, I understood.

  • 집에 오고 보니 지갑을 식당에 두고 왔더라고요.

    When I got home, I realized I'd left my wallet at the restaurant.

Compared with similar patterns

-다 보니(까) (G4-020) is about repeating/continuing an action over time; -고 보니 follows one completed action. -았/었더니 (G4-002) focuses on the result found; -고 보니 on the speaker's new realization.
