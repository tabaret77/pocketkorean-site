-ㄴ/는다고 해서

just because … (doesn't mean …)

Level 4, TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Denies that clause 1 automatically leads to clause 2. Clause 2 is usually negative: -(으)ㄴ/는 것은 아니다, -지 않다, -(으)면 안 되다.

Forming it

먹다→ 먹는다고 해서
비싸다→ 비싸다고 해서
외국인→ 외국인이라고 해서

It can also literally mean 'because someone said …': 친구가 온다고 해서 기다렸어요. Context tells you which.

Examples

  • 비싸다고 해서 다 좋은 건 아니에요.

    Just because something is expensive doesn't mean it's good.

  • 외국인이라고 해서 한국 음식을 못 먹는 건 아니에요.

    Just because someone is a foreigner doesn't mean they can't eat Korean food.

Compared with similar patterns

-ㄴ/는다고 해도 (G4-035) is a concession 'even if'. -ㄴ/는다고 하다 (G2-046) just reports speech.
