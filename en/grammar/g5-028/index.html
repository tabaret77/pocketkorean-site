-기 십상이다

be likely to (with a bad result)

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Predicts that a bad result will easily happen under a condition. Often after -(으)면 or -다가는.

Forming it

늦다→ 늦기 십상이다
실수하다→ 실수하기 십상이에요
걸리다→ 걸리기 십상이에요

Used for negative results only: ✗ 열심히 하면 합격하기 십상이에요 → 합격하기 마련이에요.

Examples

  • 이렇게 늦게 출발하면 지각하기 십상이에요.

    If you leave this late, you're likely to be late.

  • 겨울에 얇게 입으면 감기에 걸리기 십상이에요.

    If you dress lightly in winter, you can easily catch a cold.

Compared with similar patterns

-기 일쑤이다 describes a bad habit that actually happens often; -기 마련이다 / -는 법이다 state a natural, general truth (good or bad).
