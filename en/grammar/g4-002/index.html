-았/었더니

I did … and then (I found / the result was) …

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Clause 1 is something the speaker (usually 'I') did in the past; clause 2 is what they discovered afterwards or the result of that action. Clause 2 is in the past or present.

Forming it

먹다→ 먹었더니
자다→ 잤더니
연락하다→ 연락했더니
듣다→ 들었더니

Mainly used with a first-person subject. Clause 2 can't be a command, suggestion or future plan.

Examples

  • 약을 먹었더니 머리가 안 아파요.

    I took some medicine, and now my head doesn't hurt.

  • 친구 집에 갔더니 아무도 없었어요.

    I went to my friend's house, but nobody was there.

Compared with similar patterns

-더니 (G4-001) reports what the speaker watched someone or something else do. -(으)니까 (G1-027) in its discovery sense ('when I did …, I found') is close, but -았/었더니 stresses that the finding was a result of your own past action.
