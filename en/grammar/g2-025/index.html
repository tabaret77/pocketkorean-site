-(으)ㄴ/는데요

soft ending: hint, hesitation, background, polite disagreement

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Ends a sentence while leaving something unsaid: it gives background, softly refuses or disagrees, or waits for the listener's reaction. Verbs and 있다/없다 take -는데요, adjectives -(으)ㄴ데요, nouns 인데요.

Forming it

가다→ 가는데요
작다→ 작은데요
학생→ 학생인데요
먹었다→ 먹었는데요

Adjectives don't take -는: ✗ 바쁘는데요 → 바쁜데요. With a falling tone it can sound like a refusal or an objection.

Examples

  • 이 신발이 저한테 좀 작은데요.

    These shoes are a bit small for me.

  • 미안하지만 저는 지금 좀 바쁜데요.

    Sorry, but I'm a bit busy right now.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄴ/는데 links two clauses inside a sentence; -거든요 gives a reason the listener didn't know; -나요/-(으)ㄴ가요 is a soft question ending.
