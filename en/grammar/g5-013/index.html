Korean grammar patterns › Level 5

-(으)ㄹ 바에(야)

if (I) have to …, (I might as well / I'd rather …)

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Clause 1 is an unsatisfying situation; clause 2 is the better choice. Clause 2 often has 차라리, -는 게 낫다, or a command or suggestion.

Forming it

가다→ 갈 바에야
먹다→ 먹을 바에야
만들다→ 만들 바에야

Always -(으)ㄹ form: ✗ 사는 바에야.

Examples

  • 이렇게 비싸게 살 바에야 안 사는 게 낫겠어요.

    If I have to pay this much, I'd rather not buy it.

  • 어차피 늦을 바에야 천천히 가요.

    If we're going to be late anyway, let's go slowly.

Compared with similar patterns

-느니 means almost the same; -(으)ㄹ 바에야 stresses 'if it comes to that / if I'm going to … anyway'.
