-게

-ly (adverb from an adjective); so that (purpose)

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

With an adjective it makes an adverb: 예쁘게, 맛있게. With a verb it means 'so that (someone) can …', and the first clause often has a different subject from the main clause.

Forming it

크다→ 크게
맛있다→ 맛있게
듣다→ 듣게
알다→ 알게

For your own purpose with the same subject, -(으)려고 is more natural: 한국어를 배우려고 학원에 다녀요 (✗ 배우게).

Examples

  • 음식이 식기 전에 맛있게 드세요.

    Enjoy the food before it gets cold.

  • 뒤에서도 잘 들리게 크게 말해 주세요.

    Please speak loudly so people at the back can hear well.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)려고 states the subject's own intention; -도록 is similar to purpose -게 but more formal; -게끔 is an emphatic -게.
