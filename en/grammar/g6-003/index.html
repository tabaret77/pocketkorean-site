-노라면

if one keeps doing … (then eventually) (literary)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Clause 1 is an action continued over time; clause 2 is what naturally happens or is discovered as a result. Literary and poetic (essays, song lyrics).

Forming it

살다→ 사노라면
걷다→ 걷노라면
기다리다→ 기다리노라면

Verbs only, with no past or future marker: ✗ 바쁘노라면, ✗ 했노라면. ㄹ-stems drop ㄹ: 살다 → 사노라면.

Examples

  • 사노라면 언젠가 좋은 날도 오겠지요.

    If you keep on living, good days will come someday.

  • 이 길을 따라 걷노라면 작은 호수가 나옵니다.

    If you keep walking along this path, you will come to a small lake.

Compared with similar patterns

-다 보면 is the everyday spoken equivalent with the same meaning; -(으)면 is a plain 'if/when' without the sense of continuing.
