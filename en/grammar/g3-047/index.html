-(으)ㄴ/는 김에

while (doing it) anyway, since (one is) at it

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Uses the opportunity of doing clause 1 to also do clause 2, which wasn't originally planned. Verbs only; clause 2 often has 도.

Forming it

가다→ 가는 김에
나오다→ 나온 김에
하다→ 하는 김에
만들다→ 만드는 김에

Clause 1 should be something the subject does: ✗ 비가 오는 김에.

Examples

  • 시장에 가는 김에 과일도 좀 사 올게요.

    Since I'm going to the market anyway, I'll buy some fruit too.

  • 말이 나온 김에 이번 주말에 같이 영화 볼까요?

    Since we're on the subject, shall we see a movie together this weekend?

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 겸 means doing one thing for two planned purposes. -는 길에 means 'on the way to' a place.
