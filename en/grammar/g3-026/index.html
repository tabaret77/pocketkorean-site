-나 보다

it seems that, I guess (inference from what one sees)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

The speaker's guess based on evidence they observe. Verbs -나 보다, adjectives -(으)ㄴ가 보다 (in speech often -나 보다 too), nouns 인가 보다.

Forming it

오다→ 오나 봐요
바쁘다→ 바쁜가 봐요
학생→ 학생인가 봐요
먹다→ 먹었나 봐요

Not for what the speaker knows firsthand: ✗ (about your own plan) 제가 내일 가나 봐요.

Examples

  • 밖에 비가 오나 봐요. 사람들이 우산을 쓰고 있어요.

    It seems to be raining outside. People are using umbrellas.

  • 그 친구가 요즘 많이 바쁜가 봐요.

    My friend seems to be very busy these days.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄴ/는 것 같다 is a general guess and can also give one's own opinion. -(으)ㄴ/는 모양이다 is close to -나 보다 but slightly more formal.
