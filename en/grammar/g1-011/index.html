-(으)세요

please do (polite request); honorific present

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

1) A polite request or instruction: 'please do'. 2) The honorific present (-(으)시- + -어요) for describing or asking about someone you respect: 어디 가세요?

Forming it

가다→ 가세요
읽다→ 읽으세요
만들다→ 만드세요
걷다→ 걸으세요

Special honorific verbs: 먹다/마시다 → 드세요, 자다 → 주무세요, 있다 (be somewhere) → 계세요. ㄹ-stems drop ㄹ: 만들다 → 만드세요. Requests only with verbs.

Examples

  • 추우니까 따뜻한 옷을 입으세요.

    It's cold, so wear warm clothes.

  • 할머니는 매일 아침 공원을 걸으세요.

    My grandmother walks in the park every morning.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)십시오 is the formal command used in announcements and formal speech. -(으)시- is the honorific marker that -(으)세요 contains; it combines with any tense or ending.
