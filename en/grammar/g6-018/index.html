-는지라

since, as (naturally) (literary)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Gives a circumstance as the reason, implying clause 2 follows naturally from it. Literary or formal; in speech it sounds bookish.

Forming it

모르다→ 모르는지라
어리다→ 어린지라
처음→ 처음인지라
사람→ 사람인지라

Not followed by commands or suggestions: ✗ 바쁜지라 내일 오세요. Adjectives take -(으)ㄴ지라: ✗ 어리는지라.

Examples

  • 처음 해 보는 일인지라 실수가 많았습니다.

    Since it was my first time doing the work, I made a lot of mistakes.

  • 그는 길을 잘 모르는지라 한참을 헤맸다.

    He didn't know the way well, so he wandered around for a long time.

Compared with similar patterns

-아/어서 is the everyday reason connective; -기에 is a written reason, often for the speaker's reaction; -(으)므로 is a formal, logical 'because'.
