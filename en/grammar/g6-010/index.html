-기로서니

granted that …, (still, how can …?) (reproach)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Admits a reason in clause 1 but rejects it as an excuse; clause 2 is a reproachful or rhetorical question. Often with 아무리. Used in speech as well as writing.

Forming it

바쁘다→ 바쁘기로서니
늦다→ 늦었기로서니
아이→ 아이기로서니
학생→ 학생이기로서니

Clause 2 is criticism or a rhetorical question, not a neutral fact or a command.

Examples

  • 아무리 바쁘기로서니 전화 한 통 못 해요?

    No matter how busy you are, can't you even make one phone call?

  • 내가 좀 늦었기로서니 그렇게까지 화를 낼 일이에요?

    So I was a little late; is that a reason to get so angry?

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄴ들 says an action would be useless; -기로서니 rejects an excuse and blames someone. -다손 치더라도 and -ㄴ/는다고 해도 are neutral hypothetical concessions.
