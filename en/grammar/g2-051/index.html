-든지

either … or; whether … or; any- (anytime, anything)

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Offers a free choice between options (-든지 -든지): it doesn't matter which. With question words: 뭐든지 'anything', 언제든지 'anytime', 어디든지 'anywhere'. Short form -든.

Forming it

가다→ 가든지
먹다→ 먹든지
학생→ 학생이든지
언제→ 언제든지

Don't confuse it with -던지 (looking back: 얼마나 춥던지 'it was so cold'). 춥든지 ≠ 춥던지.

Examples

  • 커피를 마시든지 차를 마시든지 마음대로 하세요.

    Drink coffee or tea, whatever you like.

  • 궁금한 게 있으면 언제든지 연락하세요.

    If you have any questions, contact me anytime.

Compared with similar patterns

-거나 simply lists alternatives ('or'); -든지 stresses that any choice is fine; -아/어도 'even if' is a concession, not a choice.
