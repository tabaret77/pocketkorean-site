-듯(이)

as if, like; just as

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

V-듯(이) means 'just as, like' (doing it the same way): 다들 알듯이 'as everyone knows', 물 쓰듯 'like (spending) water'. -(으)ㄴ/는 듯(이) means 'as if' (it looks so, but isn't really): 죽은 듯이 자다.

Forming it

쓰다→ 쓰듯이
알다→ 알듯이
죽다→ 죽은 듯이

Spacing: attached directly to a stem (알듯이), but written as a separate word after a modifier (죽은 듯이).

Examples

  • 다들 알듯이 이번 주에 시험이 있어요.

    As everyone knows, there's an exam this week.

  • 아이가 너무 피곤했는지 죽은 듯이 자고 있어요.

    The child must have been really tired — he's sleeping like the dead.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄴ/는 듯하다 (G4-012) ends a sentence with 'seems'; -듯(이) connects clauses. -다시피 (G5-007) means 'as (you know/see)' and is used with verbs of knowing and perceiving.
