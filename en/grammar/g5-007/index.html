-다시피

as (you know/see); (do) almost as if

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

1) With verbs of knowing or perceiving (알다, 보다, 듣다, 느끼다), it means 'as you know / see'. 2) With other verbs + 하다, it means 'almost, practically doing': 굶다시피 하다 'practically starve'.

Forming it

알다→ 아시다시피
보다→ 보시다시피
살다→ 살다시피 하다
굶다→ 굶다시피 하다

Meaning 1 works only with knowing/perceiving verbs; meaning 2 needs 하다 after it. Not used with adjectives.

Examples

  • 아시다시피 요즘 물가가 많이 올랐어요.

    As you know, prices have gone up a lot lately.

  • 시험 기간이라 도서관에서 살다시피 했어요.

    It was exam period, so I practically lived in the library.

Compared with similar patterns

-듯(이) means 'as if, like' and compares to something else; -다시피 하다 says you nearly did the action itself.
