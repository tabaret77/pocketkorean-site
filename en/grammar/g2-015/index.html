-군요

oh, I see (reaction to a newly realized fact)

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Expresses realization or a reaction to something you have just learned or noticed, often from what the listener said. Verbs take -는군요 in the present; adjectives and 이다 take -군요.

Forming it

먹다→ 먹는군요
예쁘다→ 예쁘군요
학생→ 학생이군요
왔다→ 왔군요

Present-tense verbs need -는-: ✗ 많이 먹군요 → 많이 먹는군요. It sounds a little more formal or bookish than -네요.

Examples

  • 아, 한국 음식을 정말 좋아하시는군요.

    Ah, you really like Korean food.

  • 이 집 음식이 생각보다 맛있군요.

    The food at this place is better than I expected.

Compared with similar patterns

-네요 is the more common spoken reaction to something you directly see or feel now; -구나 is the plain (반말) form of -군요.
