-게 하다

make / let someone do; make something be

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Causative: the subject makes or allows someone (marked 에게/한테 or 을/를) do something. Works with almost any verb or adjective.

Forming it

가다→ 가게 하다
먹다→ 먹게 하다
웃다→ 웃게 하다
기다리다→ 기다리게 하다

With suffix causatives the meaning differs: 먹이다 = feed directly; 먹게 하다 = get someone to eat by themselves.

Examples

  • 엄마는 아이에게 채소를 먹게 했어요.

    The mom made the child eat vegetables.

  • 제가 늦어서 친구를 오래 기다리게 했어요.

    I was late, so I made my friend wait a long time.

Compared with similar patterns

Suffix causatives (먹이다, 입히다) exist only for some verbs and suggest direct action. -도록 하다 can also be causative but is more formal.
