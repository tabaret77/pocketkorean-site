-기만 하면

whenever …, every time; as long as (you just) …

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Every time clause 1 happens, clause 2 follows (a habit or tendency). It can also mean 'as long as you just …': 열심히 하기만 하면 돼요.

Forming it

타다→ 타기만 하면
듣다→ 듣기만 하면
보다→ 보기만 하면

Examples

  • 저는 버스를 타기만 하면 졸려요.

    Whenever I get on a bus, I get sleepy.

  • 이 노래를 듣기만 하면 고향 생각이 나요.

    Every time I hear this song, I think of my hometown.

Compared with similar patterns

-기만 하다 (G3-020) means 'only do, do nothing but'. -(으)면 (G1-028) is a plain condition; -기만 하면 stresses 'every single time' or 'that's all it takes'.
