-고 말다

end up doing (regret); will definitely do (determination)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

-고 말았다: something unwanted finally happened despite effort (regret). -고 말겠다: strong determination to achieve something.

Forming it

깨다→ 깨고 말았다
잊다→ 잊고 말았다
이기다→ 이기고 말겠다
합격하다→ 합격하고 말겠다

Examples

  • 조심했는데 결국 컵을 깨고 말았어요.

    I was careful, but I ended up breaking the cup.

  • 이번 시험에는 꼭 합격하고 말겠어요.

    I'm going to pass the exam this time no matter what.

Compared with similar patterns

-아/어 버리다 shows completion and can express relief or regret. -게 되다 is a neutral change of situation. -고 말다 stresses the outcome after resisting.
