-느니

rather than (doing that, I'd …)

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Both options are unattractive; the speaker picks clause 2 as the lesser evil. Clause 2 usually has 차라리 and -는 게 낫다 / -겠다. -느니만 못하다 means 'is worse than (doing)'.

Forming it

가다→ 가느니
먹다→ 먹느니
살다→ 사느니
기다리다→ 기다리느니

Verbs only (✗ 비싸느니).

Examples

  • 이렇게 기다리느니 차라리 걸어가는 게 낫겠어요.

    Rather than waiting like this, I'd rather walk.

  • 억지로 먹느니 안 먹는 게 나아요.

    Rather than forcing yourself to eat, it's better not to eat.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 바에(야) is almost the same; -기보다(는) is a neutral comparison without the 'both are bad' feeling.
