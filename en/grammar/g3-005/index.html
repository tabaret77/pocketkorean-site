-았/었던

that (once) did / was — completed past, often no longer true

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Noun modifier for an action completed in the past (often one time) or a past state that has since changed. 지난번에 봤던 영화 = the movie I saw last time.

Forming it

가다→ 갔던
먹다→ 먹었던
학생→ 학생이었던
가수→ 가수였던

With adjectives, -(으)ㄴ is present (작은 방 = a small room); use -았/었던 or -던 for a past state: 작았던 방.

Examples

  • 작년에 갔던 식당에 다시 가 보고 싶어요.

    I want to go again to the restaurant we went to last year.

  • 학교 다닐 때 친했던 친구를 길에서 만났어요.

    I ran into a friend I was close to in my school days.

Compared with similar patterns

-던 suggests a repeated or unfinished past action; -았/었던 suggests completion. -았/었었- is a tense in the predicate, not a noun modifier.
