-았/었-

past tense (did, was)

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Goes after the stem and before the ending to put an action or state in the past: 갔어요, 갔습니다. Choose -았- or -었- by the stem's last vowel, like -아/어요.

Forming it

가다→ 갔어요
먹다→ 먹었어요
일하다→ 일했어요
학생→ 학생이었어요

Contract the vowels: 가+았 → 갔, 오+았 → 왔, 마시+었 → 마셨. Nouns: 의사였어요 (after vowel), 학생이었어요 (after batchim).

Examples

  • 어제 친구하고 같이 저녁을 먹었어요.

    I had dinner with a friend yesterday.

  • 작년에는 저도 학생이었어요.

    Last year I was a student too.

Compared with similar patterns

-았/었었- stresses that a past situation no longer holds ('used to, but not anymore'); plain -았/었- simply says it happened in the past.
