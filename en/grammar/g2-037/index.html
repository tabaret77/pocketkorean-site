-아/어야겠다

I should, I'd better (the speaker's decision or resolve)

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Expresses the speaker's resolve that something needs to be done, often decided at that moment. Mostly about the speaker (or 'we').

Forming it

가다→ 가야겠어요
먹다→ 먹어야겠어요
쉬다→ 쉬어야겠어요
공부하다→ 공부해야겠어요

For a general rule or obligation use -아/어야 하다; -아/어야겠다 is your own judgment right now.

Examples

  • 너무 피곤해서 오늘은 일찍 자야겠어요.

    I'm so tired, I should go to bed early tonight.

  • 다음부터는 좀 더 일찍 출발해야겠어요.

    From next time, I'd better leave a bit earlier.

Compared with similar patterns

-아/어야 하다 states an obligation; -겠- alone shows intention; -(으)ㄹ게요 is a promise to the listener.
