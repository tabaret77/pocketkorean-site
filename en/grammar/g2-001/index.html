-(으)ㄴ

noun modifier for a past action (verbs): the N that someone did

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Attach to a verb stem to describe a noun with a completed action: 어제 본 영화 'the movie I saw yesterday'. Vowel stems take -ㄴ (ㄹ drops: 만들다 → 만든); consonant stems take -은.

Forming it

가다→ 간
먹다→ 먹은
만들다→ 만든
듣다→ 들은

With adjectives -(으)ㄴ is present, not past: 예쁜 꽃 = a pretty flower. For a verb's present use -는: ✗ 지금 먹은 사람 (meaning 'eating now') → 지금 먹는 사람.

Examples

  • 어제 본 영화가 정말 재미있었어요.

    The movie I saw yesterday was really fun.

  • 제가 만든 김치예요. 한번 드셔 보세요.

    This is kimchi I made. Please try some.

Compared with similar patterns

-는 = action happening now or habitually; -(으)ㄹ = future or not yet done; -았/었던 recalls a past event that is over or no longer true.
