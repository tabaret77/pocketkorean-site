-(으)ㄴ/는 줄 알다/몰랐다

thought (wrongly) that … / didn't know that …

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

-(으)ㄴ/는 줄 알았다 = I thought that… (but it wasn't so). -(으)ㄴ/는 줄 몰랐다 = I didn't know that… (but it was so). Verbs: -는 present, -(으)ㄴ past, -(으)ㄹ future; adjectives -(으)ㄴ; nouns 인.

Forming it

오다→ 오는 줄 알았어요
결혼하다→ 결혼한 줄 몰랐어요
비싸다→ 비싼 줄 몰랐어요
토요일→ 토요일인 줄 알았어요

Mostly used in the past tense (알았다 / 몰랐다).

Examples

  • 오늘이 토요일인 줄 알았어요.

    I thought today was Saturday.

  • 이 가방이 이렇게 비싼 줄 몰랐어요.

    I didn't know this bag was so expensive.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 줄 알다/모르다 after a verb means know / not know HOW to do something, not 'thought that'.
