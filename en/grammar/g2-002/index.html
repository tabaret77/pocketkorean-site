-(으)ㄹ

noun modifier for future / not yet done: the N to do, the N I will do

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Describes a noun with an action that will happen or hasn't happened yet: 내일 입을 옷 'clothes to wear tomorrow'. It also appears in many fixed patterns: -(으)ㄹ 때, -(으)ㄹ 수 있다, -(으)ㄹ 것 같다.

Forming it

가다→ 갈
먹다→ 먹을
만들다→ 만들
돕다→ 도울

ㄹ-stems don't add another ㄹ: 만들다 → 만들 (✗ 만들을). In -(으)ㄹ 때 it is not future: 어릴 때 = when I was young.

Examples

  • 내일 입을 옷을 벌써 준비했어요.

    I've already prepared the clothes I'll wear tomorrow.

  • 이번 주말에 할 일이 너무 많아요.

    I have too many things to do this weekend.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄴ marks a completed action and -는 an ongoing one; -(으)ㄹ is for what is planned, possible or not yet real. -(으)ㄹ 때 is the fixed 'when' expression.
