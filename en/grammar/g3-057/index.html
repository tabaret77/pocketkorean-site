-(으)ㄴ/는데도

even though, despite (a real fact)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Clause 2 happens contrary to what you would expect from the fact in clause 1. More emphatic: -(으)ㄴ/는데도 불구하고.

Forming it

먹다→ 먹는데도
비싸다→ 비싼데도
학생→ 학생인데도
공부하다→ 공부했는데도

Clause 1 must be a real fact, not a hypothesis: for 'even if' use -아/어도.

Examples

  • 약을 먹었는데도 감기가 안 나아요.

    Even though I took medicine, my cold isn't getting better.

  • 비가 오는데도 사람들이 많이 왔어요.

    Even though it was raining, a lot of people came.

Compared with similar patterns

-아/어도 can be hypothetical ('even if'); -(으)ㄴ/는데도 is about real facts. -(으)면서도 needs the same subject. N에도 불구하고 is the noun version.
