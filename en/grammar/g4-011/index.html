-(으)ㄴ/는 모양이다

it appears that, it looks like (inference from evidence)

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

The speaker infers something from what they see or hear, often after -는 걸 보니(까). Verbs: -(으)ㄴ (past), -는 (present), -(으)ㄹ (future guess); adjectives -(으)ㄴ.

Forming it

오다→ 온 모양이에요
자다→ 자는 모양이에요
바쁘다→ 바쁜 모양이에요
학생→ 학생인 모양이에요

Not used about your own feelings or experience: ✗ 제가 배고픈 모양이에요. It needs some evidence; for a simple opinion use -(으)ㄴ/는 것 같다.

Examples

  • 길이 젖은 걸 보니 밤에 비가 온 모양이에요.

    The street is wet, so it looks like it rained last night.

  • 옆집에 불이 다 꺼진 걸 보니 벌써 자는 모양이에요.

    All the lights next door are off, so it seems they're already asleep.

Compared with similar patterns

-나 보다 (G3-026) is very close and more casual. -(으)ㄴ/는 것 같다 (G2-006) can also express your own opinion or soften statements about yourself. -(으)ㄴ/는 듯하다 (G4-012) is more written.
