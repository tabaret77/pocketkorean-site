-(으)ㄹ 경우(에)

in case of, if (a certain situation arises); N의 경우

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Sets up a particular situation and says what happens or what to do in that case. Common in rules, instructions and announcements.

Forming it

늦다→ 늦을 경우에
오다→ 올 경우
잃어버리다→ 잃어버렸을 경우
학생→ 학생의 경우

Examples

  • 비가 올 경우에는 행사가 취소됩니다.

    If it rains, the event will be canceled.

  • 여권을 잃어버렸을 경우 바로 대사관에 연락하세요.

    If you lose your passport, contact the embassy right away.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)면 (G1-028) is the everyday 'if'; -(으)ㄹ 경우 sounds more formal and specific. -ㄴ/는다면 (G4-005) stresses that the situation is hypothetical.
