-(으)ㄹ 수밖에 없다

have no choice but to; can't help but be

Level 3, TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Says there is no other option, or that a result is natural and inevitable in the situation.

Forming it

타다→ 탈 수밖에 없다
먹다→ 먹을 수밖에 없다
듣다→ 들을 수밖에 없다
비싸다→ 비쌀 수밖에 없다

밖에 is attached to 수 and always goes with 없다: ✗ 할 수밖에 있다.

Examples

  • 버스가 끊겨서 택시를 탈 수밖에 없었어요.

    The buses had stopped running, so I had no choice but to take a taxi.

  • 매일 연습했으니까 잘할 수밖에 없죠.

    She practiced every day, so of course she's good.

Compared with similar patterns

-지 않을 수 없다 (double negative) is more formal and emphatic; -지 않으면 안 되다 expresses obligation. -(으)ㄹ 수밖에 없다 focuses on having no alternative or on inevitability.
