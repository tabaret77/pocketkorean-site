-(으)면 되다

all you have to do is; it's enough to; you can just

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Says that one simple condition is enough — a common way to give instructions or directions. The negative -(으)면 안 되다 means 'must not'.

Forming it

가다→ 가면 돼요
먹다→ 먹으면 돼요
만들다→ 만들면 돼요
걷다→ 걸으면 돼요

It doesn't mean 'must': it says this single action is enough.

Examples

  • 여기에 이름하고 전화번호만 쓰시면 돼요.

    You just need to write your name and phone number here.

  • 역까지 10분만 걸으면 돼요.

    It's only a ten-minute walk to the station.

Compared with similar patterns

-아/어야 하다 is obligation; -아/어도 되다 gives permission; -(으)려면 sets the goal ('if you want to …'), and -(으)면 되다 often answers it.
