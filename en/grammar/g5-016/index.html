-고서(야)

after doing; (only) after doing (did …)

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

-고서 stresses that clause 1 is finished before clause 2 (like -고 나서). -고서야 means 'only after … (finally)'; with a negative + rhetorical question it means 'unless … how could': 노력하지 않고서야 어떻게 성공하겠어요?

Forming it

먹다→ 먹고서
보다→ 보고서야
듣다→ 듣고서야

Same subject in both clauses; no tense marker before -고서.

Examples

  • 그는 인사도 하지 않고서 나가 버렸어요.

    He left without even saying goodbye.

  • 직접 겪고서야 그 말의 뜻을 알게 됐어요.

    Only after experiencing it myself did I understand what those words meant.

Compared with similar patterns

-고 나서 is neutral 'after'; -고서 is a bit more emphatic. -아/어서야 also means 'only when/after' but attaches to adjectives and time expressions too (열 시가 넘어서야).
