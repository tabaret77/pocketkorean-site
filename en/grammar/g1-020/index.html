-고 있다

be doing (progressive); be wearing

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

1) An action in progress: 'be doing'. It can also describe an ongoing situation (요즘 서울에 살고 있어요). 2) With wearing verbs (입다, 신다, 쓰다, 끼다, 메다) it also means 'be wearing'.

Forming it

먹다→ 먹고 있어요
공부하다→ 공부하고 있어요
입다→ 입고 있어요

Verbs only, not adjectives. For a state that results from a completed action use -아/어 있다: 의자에 앉아 있어요 (is seated), 문이 열려 있어요.

Examples

  • 지금 친구를 기다리고 있어요.

    I'm waiting for a friend now.

  • 동생은 오늘 하얀 셔츠를 입고 있어요.

    My younger sibling is wearing a white shirt today.

Compared with similar patterns

-아/어 있다 describes the resulting state (앉아 있다, 문이 열려 있다); -는 중이다 means 'in the middle of' and stresses being busy with something.
