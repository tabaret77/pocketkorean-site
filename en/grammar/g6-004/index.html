-노라(고)

I (hereby) declare that …; saying that I … (literary)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

A solemn, literary first-person declaration of what the speaker did or will do (왔노라, 하겠노라). With -고 it quotes such a vow (…하겠노라고 다짐하다/약속하다). 하노라고 했는데 means 'I did it as best I could (but)'.

Forming it

하다→ 하노라
오다→ 왔노라
가다→ 가겠노라고
살다→ 사노라

The subject is the speaker, or a quoted person speaking about himself. Not used with adjectives in modern Korean.

Examples

  • 나는 끝까지 이 길을 가겠노라고 다짐했다.

    I vowed that I would follow this path to the end.

  • 딴에는 열심히 하노라고 했는데 결과가 좋지 않았어요.

    I did my best, as I saw it, but the result wasn't good.

Compared with similar patterns

-ㄴ/는다고 하다 is the neutral way to report speech; -(으)리라 expresses will or confident prediction, while -노라 announces or declares.
