-지요

…, right? (seeking agreement); of course

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Seeks agreement or confirmation about something the speaker thinks the listener also knows: '…, right?'. Said flatly, it states something as obvious, or softly offers to do something (제가 하죠). In speech it is usually 죠.

Forming it

덥다→ 덥지요?
맛있다→ 맛있죠?
가다→ 가지요
학생→ 학생이지요?

Examples

  • 오늘 날씨가 정말 많이 덥지요?

    It's really hot today, isn't it?

  • 그 영화 정말 재미있었죠?

    That movie was really fun, wasn't it?

Compared with similar patterns

-잖아요 reminds the listener of something they know, often as a reason. -네요 reacts to something just noticed, while -지요 assumes it is already known.
