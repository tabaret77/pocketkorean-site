-(으)ㄹ 뿐이다

only, merely; it's just that … (nothing more)

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Limits the situation to just this, often to play something down or explain: 'it's only that …'. Past: -았/었을 뿐이다.

Forming it

하다→ 할 뿐이에요
듣다→ 들을 뿐이에요
하다→ 했을 뿐이에요
친구→ 친구일 뿐이에요

Examples

  • 감사하긴요, 저는 제가 할 일을 했을 뿐이에요.

    No need to thank me — I only did what I had to do.

  • 그 사람과는 그냥 친한 친구일 뿐이에요.

    He's just a close friend, nothing more.

Compared with similar patterns

-기만 하다 (G3-020) means 'do nothing but' one action. -(으)ㄹ 따름이다 (G5-036) is a more formal version. -(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 (G3-024) means 'not only … but also'.
