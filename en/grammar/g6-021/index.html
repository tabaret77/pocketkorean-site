-(으)ㄴ/는 양

as if …, pretending to … (literary)

Level 6, TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Describes behaving as though something were true when it isn't (or isn't certain). Often with 마치, and also as -는 양하다 'to pretend'. Literary.

Forming it

모르다→ 모르는 양
보다→ 본 양
잘나다→ 잘난 양
주인→ 주인인 양

Examples

  • 그는 마치 자기가 주인인 양 행동했다.

    He acted as if he were the owner.

  • 그녀는 아무것도 모르는 양 시치미를 뗐다.

    She pretended not to know anything.

Compared with similar patterns

-듯(이) and -(으)ㄴ/는 듯하다 are broader ('like, seem') and more common; -(으)ㄴ/는 척하다 is the everyday 'pretend'. -는 양 is literary and stresses that the appearance is false.

Related patterns

  • -듯(이) as if, like; just as
  • -(으)ㄴ/는 척하다 pretend to (do / be)
  • -(으)ㄴ/는 듯하다 seem, appear (softer, more written than -(으)ㄴ/는 것 같다)
