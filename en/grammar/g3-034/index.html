-(으)ㄹ 뻔하다

almost, nearly (did — but it didn't happen)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Something nearly happened but in the end did not. Used in the past tense (-(으)ㄹ 뻔했다), often with 하마터면.

Forming it

넘어지다→ 넘어질 뻔했다
늦다→ 늦을 뻔했다
잊다→ 잊을 뻔했다
울다→ 울 뻔했다

Present tense is not used: ✗ 늦을 뻔해요. Don't confuse with -(으)ㄹ 게 뻔하다 (it's obvious that).

Examples

  • 길이 미끄러워서 넘어질 뻔했어요.

    The road was slippery, so I almost fell.

  • 알람을 못 들어서 회사에 늦을 뻔했어요.

    I didn't hear my alarm, so I was almost late for work.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 게 뻔하다 predicts that something will obviously happen; -(으)ㄹ 뻔했다 says it almost happened but didn't.
