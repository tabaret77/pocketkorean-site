-기(가) 무섭게

no sooner than, the (very) moment

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Clause 2 happens the very instant clause 1 happens, often showing eagerness or a rush. It is an exaggerated, vivid expression, used for past events or repeated habits.

Forming it

받다→ 받기가 무섭게
끝나다→ 끝나기 무섭게
눕다→ 눕기가 무섭게

Not used with commands or suggestions: ✗ 도착하기가 무섭게 전화하세요 → 도착하자마자 … .

Examples

  • 월급을 받기가 무섭게 다 써 버렸어요.

    No sooner had I gotten my salary than I spent it all.

  • 새 휴대폰이 나오기 무섭게 다 팔렸어요.

    The new phone sold out the moment it came out.

Compared with similar patterns

-자마자 is neutral 'as soon as' and allows commands; -자 is narrative; -기가 무섭게 is the strongest and most vivid.
