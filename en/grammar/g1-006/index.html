-ㅂ/습니까?

formal polite question ending

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

The formal question ending, the question partner of -ㅂ/습니다. Heard in service situations, interviews, classrooms and announcements; usually answered with -ㅂ/습니다.

Forming it

가다→ 갑니까?
있다→ 있습니까?
알다→ 압니까?
학생→ 학생입니까?

ㄹ-stems drop ㄹ: 알다 → 압니까? (✗ 알습니까?). Past: -았/었습니까? (먹었습니까?).

Examples

  • 실례지만, 화장실이 어디에 있습니까?

    Excuse me, where is the restroom?

  • 손님, 무엇을 주문하시겠습니까?

    Sir/Ma'am, what would you like to order?

Compared with similar patterns

-ㅂ/습니다 is the statement form. In everyday polite speech, a question is just -아/어요? with rising intonation.
