-(으)ㄹ세라

lest, for fear that … (literary)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Clause 1 is something the subject worries might happen; clause 2 is what they do (quickly, carefully) to prevent it. Literary or a bit old-fashioned.

Forming it

깨다→ 깰세라
늦다→ 늦을세라
듣다→ 들을세라
알다→ 알세라

Clause 2 cannot be a command or suggestion: ✗ 늦을세라 빨리 가세요 → 늦을까 봐 …(or 늦지 않게 빨리 가세요).

Examples

  • 아이가 깰세라 살금살금 방을 나왔어요.

    I tiptoed out of the room for fear the child would wake up.

  • 누가 볼세라 그는 편지를 얼른 주머니에 넣었다.

    Lest anyone see it, he quickly put the letter in his pocket.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ까 봐(서) is the everyday way to express this worry; -도록 states the goal positively ('so that the child doesn't wake').
