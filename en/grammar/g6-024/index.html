-(으)소서

please … (prayer, supplication; highest respect)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

The most respectful command/request form, used in prayers, wishes for the dead, and to a king in historical dramas. Often with 부디 or 아무쪼록.

Forming it

평안하다→ 평안하소서
받다→ 받으소서
잠들다→ 잠드소서
지켜 주다→ 지켜 주소서

Not for real requests to people today: ✗ 선생님, 여기 앉으소서 → 앉으십시오.

Examples

  • 신이시여, 우리 가족을 지켜 주소서.

    O God, please protect our family.

  • 부디 그곳에서 평안히 잠드소서.

    May you rest in peace there.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)십시오 is the formal command for real situations; -(으)십사 하다 politely makes or reports a request ('I ask that you …').
