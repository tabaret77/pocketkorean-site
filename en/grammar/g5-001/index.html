-기에

since, because (written / formal)

Level 5, TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Clause 1 gives the reason or the situation that prompted the action in clause 2. It sounds more written or formal than -아/어서; in speech people usually say -길래.

Forming it

막히다→ 막히기에
좋다→ 좋기에
했다→ 했기에
학생→ 학생이기에

Clause 2 cannot be a command or suggestion: ✗ 비가 오기에 우산을 가져가세요 → 비가 오니까 … .

Examples

  • 길이 많이 막히기에 지하철을 탔어요.

    The roads were very congested, so I took the subway.

  • 친구가 아프다고 하기에 약을 사다 줬어요.

    My friend said they were sick, so I bought them some medicine.

Compared with similar patterns

-길래 is the colloquial version with the same meaning. -아/어서 is the neutral everyday 'because'. -(으)므로 is formal written logic (reports, papers) and lacks the 'seeing this, I did …' nuance.

Related patterns

  • -길래 since, because (I saw / heard that …) — reason for my action
  • -아/어서 because, so (reason); and then (sequence)
  • -(으)므로 because, since (formal / written)
