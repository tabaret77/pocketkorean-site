-아/어지다

become (change of state with adjectives); be done (passive)

Level 2 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

With adjectives it shows a change: 추워지다 'get cold', 커지다 'get bigger'. With some verbs it makes a passive or 'it works / gets done' meaning: 만들어지다, 잘 써지다.

Forming it

크다→ 커지다
따뜻하다→ 따뜻해지다
예쁘다→ 예뻐지다
만들다→ 만들어지다

Write it as one word: 예뻐지다 (✗ 예뻐 지다). Don't mix up 좋아지다 'get better' and 좋아하다 'like'.

Examples

  • 봄이 되니까 날씨가 따뜻해졌어요.

    Now that it's spring, the weather has gotten warm.

  • 이 볼펜은 글씨가 정말 잘 써져요.

    This pen writes really well.

Compared with similar patterns

-게 되다 is a change in situation or action caused by circumstances; many common verbs use passive suffixes instead (보이다, 열리다); -아/어 보이다 means 'look (adj)', not 'become'.
