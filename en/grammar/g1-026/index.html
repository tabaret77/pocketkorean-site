Korean grammar patterns › Level 1

-네요

oh, …! (reaction on noticing something)

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Expresses surprise or a feeling about something you notice right now, often in reaction to what you see, hear or taste. Very common for compliments and sympathetic reactions.

Forming it

크다→ 크네요
맛있다→ 맛있네요
멀다→ 머네요
학생→ 학생이네요

ㄹ-stems drop ㄹ: 멀다 → 머네요, 살다 → 사네요 (✗ 멀네요). Not for facts you already knew before.

Examples

  • 와, 이 김치찌개 정말 맛있네요!

    Wow, this kimchi stew is really good!

  • 벌써 저녁 일곱 시네요. 시간이 빨리 가요.

    It's already seven in the evening. Time flies.

Compared with similar patterns

-군요 also expresses realization but sounds more like 'I see' about new information and is a bit more formal; -지요 assumes the listener already knows and seeks agreement.
