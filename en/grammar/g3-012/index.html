-았/었다가

did … and then (undid it / something else happened)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

The first action is completed, and then the opposite action follows or something happens as a result (typically: went somewhere and then…). Same subject in both clauses.

Forming it

가다→ 갔다가
입다→ 입었다가
켜다→ 켰다가
사다→ 샀다가

Often pairs of opposites: 입었다가 벗다, 켰다가 끄다, 앉았다가 일어나다.

Examples

  • 옷을 샀다가 마음에 안 들어서 바꿨어요.

    I bought the clothes, but didn't like them, so I exchanged them.

  • 도서관에 갔다가 문이 닫혀서 그냥 왔어요.

    I went to the library, but it was closed, so I just came back.

Compared with similar patterns

-다가 switches while the first action is still going on; -았/었다가 switches after it is completed. -(으)려다가 means one was about to do something but didn't.
