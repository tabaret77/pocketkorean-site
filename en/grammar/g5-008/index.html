-자

as soon as, when (… then); N이자 'N and also'

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Clause 2 happens right after clause 1, often as its result or as something noticed. Typical in narration and writing. N이자 N means 'both N and N' (가수이자 배우 'a singer and actor').

Forming it

열다→ 열자
먹다→ 먹자
도착하다→ 도착하자
가수→ 가수이자

No tense before -자 (✗ 열었자). Clause 2 is usually past and cannot be a command or suggestion: ✗ 도착하자 전화하세요 → 도착하자마자 … .

Examples

  • 문을 열자 고양이가 뛰어나갔어요.

    As soon as I opened the door, the cat ran out.

  • 버스에서 내리자 비가 쏟아지기 시작했어요.

    As soon as I got off the bus, it started pouring rain.

Compared with similar patterns

-자마자 stresses immediacy and can be followed by commands; -자 is more narrative and often implies cause. -기(가) 무섭게 is an exaggerated 'the very moment'.
