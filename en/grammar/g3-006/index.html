-았/었었-

had done / used to (past situation that is no longer true)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Double past marker: shows that something happened or was true in the past but is cut off from the present (it has changed or ended). 여기 왔었어요 = I came here before (but not now).

Forming it

살다→ 살았었어요
먹다→ 먹었었어요
하다→ 했었어요
길다→ 길었었어요

Not for simple recent actions: 어제 밥을 먹었어요 (✗ 먹었었어요 sounds odd). Don't use it for states that still continue.

Examples

  • 어렸을 때 바닷가 근처에 살았었어요.

    When I was young, I lived near the sea.

  • 예전에는 머리가 길었었는데 지금은 짧아요.

    I used to have long hair, but now it's short.

Compared with similar patterns

-았/었- is the simple past (the result may still continue); -았/었었- stresses contrast with the present. -았/었던 is the noun-modifier form.
