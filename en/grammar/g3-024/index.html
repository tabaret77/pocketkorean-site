-(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라

not only … but also

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Adds a second point to the first; clause 2 often has 도. Nouns: N뿐만 아니라. Past: -았/었을 뿐만 아니라.

Forming it

싸다→ 쌀 뿐만 아니라
맛있다→ 맛있을 뿐만 아니라
춥다→ 추울 뿐만 아니라
노래→ 노래뿐만 아니라

Both points should go in the same direction (both good or both bad).

Examples

  • 이 식당은 음식이 맛있을 뿐만 아니라 값도 싸요.

    This restaurant not only has delicious food but is also cheap.

  • 그 가수는 노래뿐만 아니라 춤도 잘 춰요.

    That singer is good not only at singing but also at dancing.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄴ/는 데다(가) is more colloquial ('on top of that'). -(으)ㄹ뿐더러 is written style. N은/는 물론 means 'N, of course, and also'.
