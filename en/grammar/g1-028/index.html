-(으)면

if; when(ever)

Level 1, TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Expresses a condition ('if') or a general/repeated situation ('whenever'). Any sentence type can follow, including commands and suggestions.

Forming it

가다→ 가면
먹다→ 먹으면
춥다→ 추우면
학생→ 학생이면

For a single past event use -았/었을 때, not -(으)면: ✗ 어제 집에 가면 → 어제 집에 갔을 때. Don't add future markers for a simple condition: ✗ 비가 오겠으면 → 비가 오면.

Examples

  • 시간이 있으면 같이 영화 봐요.

    If you have time, let's watch a movie together.

  • 봄이 되면 꽃이 많이 피어요.

    When spring comes, lots of flowers bloom.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ 때 just marks the time ('at the time when'); -(으)면 is a condition. -(으)면 되다 means 'you just need to'.
