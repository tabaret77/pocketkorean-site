-(으)ㄹ걸요

I guess …, probably … (soft guess, often against expectation)

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Gives a mild, uncertain guess, often gently correcting what the listener expects. Said with rising intonation. Casual: -(으)ㄹ걸.

Forming it

오다→ 올걸요
많다→ 많을걸요
학생→ 학생일걸요
닫다→ 닫았을걸요

Not for the speaker's intention: ✗ 제가 할걸요 → 할게요.

Examples

  • 지금 가면 아마 문을 닫았을걸요.

    If you go now, they've probably already closed.

  • 그 식당은 주말에 사람이 많을걸요.

    That restaurant is probably crowded on weekends.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ걸 (그랬다) with falling intonation expresses regret ('I should have'). -(으)ㄹ게요 is a promise or the speaker's intention.
