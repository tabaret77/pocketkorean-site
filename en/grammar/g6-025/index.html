-는 족족

every time … (each one), as soon as …

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Each time something is done or appears, the same thing happens to every item, often that it is used up or disappears (다 쓰다, 다 팔리다). Clause 2 is usually past or habitual.

Forming it

벌다→ 버는 족족
먹다→ 먹는 족족
만들다→ 만드는 족족
보이다→ 보이는 족족

Verbs only, and only the present form -는: ✗ 번 족족, ✗ 비싼 족족.

Examples

  • 그는 돈을 버는 족족 다 써 버려요.

    Every time he earns money, he spends all of it.

  • 빵을 만드는 족족 손님들이 다 사 갔어요.

    As fast as we made bread, the customers bought it all.

Compared with similar patterns

-는 대로 and -자마자 mean 'as soon as' for one event; -는 족족 stresses every single time / every item. -(으)ㄹ라치면 means 'whenever one is about to' and something interferes.
