-기 나름이다

depend on how one does it

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

The result is not fixed; it depends on the way someone does the action. N 나름이다 = 'it depends on the N' (사람 나름이다 'it depends on the person').

Forming it

생각하다→ 생각하기 나름이다
하다→ 하기 나름이에요
사람→ 사람 나름이에요

Examples

  • 행복은 생각하기 나름이에요.

    Happiness depends on how you think about it.

  • 아이는 부모가 가르치기 나름이에요.

    How a child turns out depends on how the parents teach them.

Compared with similar patterns

N에 달려 있다 is a general 'depend on N'; -기 나름이다 stresses that the outcome depends on someone's attitude or effort.
