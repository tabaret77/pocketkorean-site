Korean grammar patterns › Level 3

-(으)ㄹ수록

the more …, the more …

Level 3 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

As the degree of clause 1 increases, clause 2 changes along with it. -(으)면 -(으)ㄹ수록 repeats the word for emphasis.

Forming it

보다→ 볼수록
먹다→ 먹을수록
많다→ 많을수록
멀다→ 멀수록

Written as one word (볼수록). ㄷ irregular: 듣다 → 들을수록; ㄹ-stem: 멀다 → 멀수록.

Examples

  • 한국어는 공부하면 할수록 재미있어요.

    The more I study Korean, the more fun it is.

  • 여행은 사람이 많을수록 더 즐거워요.

    The more people on a trip, the more enjoyable it is.
