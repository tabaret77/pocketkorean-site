-(으)ㄹ까요?

shall we/I …?; do you think …?

Level 1 · TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

1) With 'we' or 'I' as subject: a suggestion or offer, 'shall we/I…?'. 2) Asking the listener's opinion or guess: 'do you think…?'.

Forming it

가다→ 갈까요?
먹다→ 먹을까요?
만들다→ 만들까요?
걷다→ 걸을까요?

Answer a 'shall we…?' with -아/어요 or -(으)ㅂ시다 (좋아요, 같이 가요), not with another -(으)ㄹ까요. ㄹ-stems add only -ㄹ까요: 만들까요?

Examples

  • 날씨가 좋은데 공원에서 좀 걸을까요?

    The weather is nice. Shall we take a walk in the park?

  • 내일 아침에도 비가 많이 올까요?

    Do you think it will rain a lot tomorrow morning too?

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)ㄹ래요? asks what the listener wants ('do you want to…?'); -(으)ㅂ시다 is a formal 'let's' statement, not a question.
