-건만

although …, (yet) (literary; expectation not met)

Level 6 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

Clause 1 is a real fact that should lead to a certain result; clause 2 shows that result didn't happen, often with disappointment or regret. Literary; a contracted form of -건마는.

Forming it

준비하다→ 준비했건만
알다→ 알건만
휴가→ 휴가이건만
봄→ 봄이건만

Examples

  • 그렇게 열심히 준비했건만 결과는 좋지 않았다.

    I prepared so hard, but the result was not good.

  • 벌써 봄이건만 아직도 바람이 차갑다.

    It's already spring, yet the wind is still cold.

Compared with similar patterns

-지만 is a neutral 'but'; -(으)ㄴ/는데도 is the spoken 'even though'. -(으)련만 is about a hypothetical expected result ('would …, but'), while -건만 starts from an actual fact.
