-아/어서

because, so (reason); and then (sequence)

Level 1, TOPIK I · beginner

How to use it

Links two clauses. Clause 1 is the reason for clause 2, or happens first and leads into it (same subject, closely connected actions). The tense goes on the last verb.

Forming it

가다→ 가서
먹다→ 먹어서
공부하다→ 공부해서
학생→ 학생이라서

Not used before commands or suggestions: ✗ 비가 와서 우산을 가져가세요 → 비가 오니까 … . Never put -았/었- before -아/어서: ✗ 늦었어서.

Examples

  • 비가 와서 집에 있었어요.

    It rained, so I stayed home.

  • 친구를 만나서 같이 영화를 봤어요.

    I met my friend and we watched a movie together.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)니까 can be followed by commands and suggestions, but sounds odd with thanks and apologies (✗ 늦으니까 죄송합니다). -기 때문에 sounds more formal and stresses the cause. -고 just lists or orders actions without the tight link.

Related patterns
