-(으)ㄹ 법하다

it is likely, it makes sense that

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

The speaker judges that something is reasonable or quite likely given the circumstances.

Forming it

나다→ 날 법하다
있다→ 있을 법해요
알다→ 알 법해요
들었다→ 들었을 법해요

Do not confuse with -는 법이다 (general truth). Always -(으)ㄹ: ✗ 고프는 법해요.

Examples

  • 그 정도면 화가 날 법해요.

    Anyone would get angry at that.

  • 누구에게나 있을 법한 일이에요.

    It's something that could happen to anyone.

Compared with similar patterns

-는 법이다 states a general rule; -(으)ㄴ/는 듯하다 says 'it seems'; -(으)ㄹ 법하다 says 'it would be natural / plausible'.
