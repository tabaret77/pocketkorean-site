-ㄴ/는다니(요)

What, …?! (surprise or disbelief at what was said)

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Repeats or refers to what someone just said or a fact just learned, showing surprise, disbelief or indignation. -ㄴ/는다니 can also continue the sentence: …다니 믿을 수 없어요.

Forming it

그만두다→ 그만둔다니요
끝나다→ 끝났다니
싫다→ 싫다니요
거짓말→ 거짓말이라니요

For questions, commands and suggestions: -냐니요, -(으)라니요, -자니요.

Examples

  • 회사를 그만둔다니요? 그게 정말이에요?

    You're quitting your job? Is that really true?

  • 벌써 다 끝났다니 믿을 수가 없어요.

    I can't believe it's already all over.

Compared with similar patterns

-ㄴ/는다면서요 (G3-060) calmly checks something you heard. -ㄴ/는다고요 (G3-041) repeats to confirm or emphasize. -ㄴ/는다니까요 (G4-056) insists on what you yourself said.
