-아/어 내다

manage to …, finally accomplish (through effort)

Level 4 · TOPIK II · intermediate

How to use it

Emphasizes that something difficult was done or endured to the end through effort. Common with 참다, 견디다, 이기다, 버티다.

Forming it

참다→ 참아 내다
이기다→ 이겨 내다
견디다→ 견뎌 내다

Some combinations have become single words written without a space: 해내다, 찾아내다, 알아내다.

Examples

  • 그는 어려움을 이겨 내고 꿈을 이루었어요.

    He overcame his difficulties and achieved his dream.

  • 아이는 주사가 아팠지만 울지 않고 끝까지 참아 냈어요.

    The shot hurt, but the child endured it to the end without crying.

Compared with similar patterns

-고 말다 (G3-049) emphasizes the final result, often with regret; -아/어 내다 stresses effort and accomplishment. -아/어 버리다 (G2-023) means completion with relief or regret, not effort.
