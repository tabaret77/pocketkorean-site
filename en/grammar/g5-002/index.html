-(으)니

since, as (reason); when (I did …, I found)

Level 5 · TOPIK II · advanced

How to use it

A shorter, more written/literary form of -(으)니까. It gives a reason, or (with a past clause 2) says what the speaker discovered after doing something. Common in notices, proverbs and writing.

Forming it

가다→ 가니
먹다→ 먹으니
살다→ 사니
학생→ 학생이니

ㄹ-stem verbs drop ㄹ: 열다 → 여니, 살다 → 사니 (✗ 열으니). For the discovery meaning, clause 2 is past and clause 1 has no tense marker.

Examples

  • 봄이 오니 꽃이 피기 시작했어요.

    Spring came, so the flowers started to bloom.

  • 집에 도착하니 벌써 밤 열 시였어요.

    When I got home, it was already 10 p.m.

Compared with similar patterns

-(으)니까 is the everyday spoken form; -(으)니 means the same but sounds more written or literary, e.g. in notices: 날씨가 추우니 따뜻하게 입으십시오.
