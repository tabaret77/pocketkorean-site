Hobby words in Korean: how to talk about what you do for fun

2026-10-23

Phrase from this story: 취미가 뭐예요? = What's your hobby?Search a word

Sooner or later, at a language exchange, a first day at work or a blind date in a K-drama, someone will ask you 취미가 뭐예요? (Chwimiga mwoyeyo?) – What's your hobby? It's one of the most common small-talk questions in Korean. 취미 (chwimi) means hobby, and the cooler weather of late autumn is a good time to pick one up. Many people call autumn 독서의 계절 (doksseoui gyejeol [독써의 계절]) – the season of reading – because the clear, cool days make it easy to settle down with a book. Here are the words and patterns you need to talk about what you do for fun.

24 hobby words

Many hobbies are nouns that become verbs with 하다 (hada) – to do. Others are a verb turned into a noun with -기 (-gi), like 사진 찍기, "taking photos".

산책 sanchaek going for a walk

캠핑 kaemping camping
낚시 nakssi [낙씨] fishing
여행 yeohaeng travel
사진 찍기 sajin jjikki [사진 찍끼] taking photos

피아노를 치다 pianoreul chida to play the piano
기타를 치다 gitareul chida to play the guitar

Instruments you strike or pluck, like the piano and guitar, use 치다 (chida), not 하다.

Two ways to answer

1. 제 취미는 + noun + 이에요/예요. The simplest answer just names the hobby:

  • 제 취미는 등산이에요. (Je chwimineun deungsanieyo.) – My hobby is hiking.
  • 제 취미는 요리예요. (Je chwimineun yoriyeyo.) – My hobby is cooking.

For an action, turn the verb into a noun with -기. Take the stem and add -기: 읽다 → 읽기, 찍다 → 찍기.

  • 제 취미는 책 읽기예요. (Je chwimineun chaek ilkkiyeyo. [책 일끼예요.]) – My hobby is reading books.
  • 제 취미는 사진 찍기예요. (Je chwimineun sajin jjikkiyeyo. [제 취미는 사진 찍끼예요.]) – My hobby is taking photos.

2. -는 것을 좋아해요. To say "I like doing …", add -는 것을 좋아해요 to the verb stem. In speech, 것을 usually shortens to 걸.

Verb + -는 것을 좋아해요 Meaning
요리하다 (yorihada) 요리하는 것을 좋아해요 (yorihaneun geoseul joahaeyo) I like cooking
걷다 (geotta [걷따]) 걷는 걸 좋아해요 (geonneun geol joahaeyo) I like walking
듣다 (deutta [듣따]) 음악 듣는 걸 좋아해요 (eumak deunneun geol joahaeyo) I like listening to music
만들다 (mandeulda) 만드는 걸 좋아해요 (mandeuneun geol joahaeyo) I like making things
그리다 (geurida) 그림 그리는 걸 좋아해요 (geurim geurineun geol joahaeyo) I like drawing

Two notes: stems ending in ㄹ drop it before -는 (만들다 → 만드는), and a final ㄷ or ㄱ before 는 is pronounced as ㄴ or ㅇ: 걷는 [건는], 듣는 [든는].

Asking more

  • 시간 있을 때 뭐 해요? (Sigan isseul ttae mwo haeyo?) – What do you do when you have time?
  • 주말에 보통 뭐 해요? (Jumare botong mwo haeyo?) – What do you usually do at the weekend?
  • 얼마나 자주 해요? (Eolmana jaju haeyo?) – How often do you do it?
  • 같이 할래요? (Gachi hallaeyo?) – Shall we do it together?

And to answer how often: 매일 (maeil) – every day, 일주일에 두 번 (iljuire du beon) – twice a week, 가끔 (gakkeum) – sometimes.

Watch the pronunciation

  • 독서 is said [독써]: the ㅅ after a final ㄱ turns tense.
  • 낚시 is said [낙씨] (naksi).
  • 악기 is said [악끼] (akgi).

저는 ___는 걸 좋아해요. (Jeoneun neun geol joahaeyo.) – I like ing. Write three sentences about your own hobbies, then ask someone 취미가 뭐예요?
