Support

Help for the Pocket German app and website. Questions or problems? Write to hello@pocketkorean.com — we usually answer within 1–2 days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I buy the app?

Pocket German is a paid app: you pay once on the App Store and download it. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions; every level and feature is included.

I have a new phone or deleted the app.

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to buy and download Pocket German again. You already own it, so you are not charged again.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

There is no sound.

Check the volume, Bluetooth headphones and your internet connection. If it still fails, close the app completely and open it again.

I changed phones and my progress is gone.

Your progress is saved only on your device — we keep no accounts or server copies, so it cannot be moved between devices.

What is free on the web?

A1 words and the topic word lists are free to try in the web app. The full set of levels and features is in the paid app.
