Today's words · 🎵 Music & songs

Talk about your favourite songs: 10 German music words

A1A2B1B2

‘What are you listening to at the moment?’ — one question that can keep you chatting with a German friend for an hour.

You're in a German friend's car and a song you've never heard comes on the radio. Your friend sings along happily, but you can't even get out ‘What's this song? Who's the singer?’ in German.

Today's ten words are what you need to talk about music. Your favourite singer, their voice, the melody, the lyrics — with these, you can start swapping music tastes. Headphones on! 🎧

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. die Musik — music
  2. das Lied — song
  3. singen — to sing
  4. der Sänger — singer
  5. die Stimme — voice; vote
  6. die Melodie — melody, tune
  7. der Text — text
  8. der Rhythmus — rhythm; cycle
  9. der Hit — hit (song); bestseller
  10. das Album — album; photo album

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

die Musik

[moo-ZEEK]music

((no pl.))

📌 Ich höre gern Musik.

→ I like listening to music.

💡 Stress at the end: moo-ZEEK 🎵 Say ‘MEW-zik’ and Germans will look puzzled.

Bonus Musik hören = to listen to music · Musik machen = to make/play music

02

das Lied

[LEET]song

(Pl. die Lieder)

📌 Kennst du dieses Lied?

→ Do you know this song?

💡 Classical music fans know Lieder, art songs 🎼 Short and simple: ‘leet’.

⚠ Plural: die Lieder. The final d sounds like t.

03

singen

[ZING-en]to sing

(singt – hat gesungen)

📌 Sie singt in einem Chor.

→ She sings in a choir.

💡 English sing's twin 🎤 But the s is a soft z: ‘zing-en’.

⚠ Irregular: sang – hat gesungen

04

der Sänger

[ZENG-er]singer

(Pl. die Sänger (f: die Sängerin))

📌 Die Sängerin hat eine tolle Stimme.

→ The singer has a great voice.

💡 singen + -er = someone who sings 🎤 The i changes to ä.

Bonus A female singer is die Sängerin.

05

die Stimme

[SHTIM-muh]voice; vote

(Pl. die Stimmen)

📌 Sie hat eine schöne Stimme.

→ She has a beautiful voice.

💡 Family of Stimmung (mood) 🗣️ A voice sets the mood!

Bonus It also means a vote: Jede Stimme zählt. = Every vote counts.

06

die Melodie

[meh-loh-DEE]melody, tune

(Pl. die Melodien)

📌 Diese Melodie geht mir nicht aus dem Kopf.

→ I can't get this tune out of my head.

💡 Stress on the very end: -DIE 🎶 Loanwords in -ie are usually die.

Bonus A tune stuck in your head = der Ohrwurm — English earworm comes from it!

07

der Text

[TEKST]text

(Pl. die Texte)

📌 Lesen Sie den Text.

→ Read the text.

💡 Just English text 📝 For songs, it means the lyrics.

Bonus lyrics = der Songtext · Ich kenne den Text nicht. = I don't know the words.

08

der Rhythmus

[RÜT-mooss]rhythm; cycle

(Pl. die Rhythmen)

📌 Sie tanzt im Rhythmus der Musik.

→ She dances to the rhythm of the music.

💡 Rh, th, y — scary spelling, simple sound: ‘RÜT-mooss’ 🥁

⚠ Plural: die Rhythmen · and watch the two h's.

09

der Hit

[HIT]hit (song); bestseller

(Pl. die Hits)

📌 Dieses Lied war in den 80er-Jahren ein großer Hit.

→ This song was a big hit in the 80s.

💡 Straight from English 💥 A song that hits the charts.

Bonus summer hit = der Sommerhit

10

das Album

[AL-boom]album; photo album

(Pl. die Alben)

📌 Die Band hat gerade ein neues Album herausgebracht.

→ The band has just released a new album.

💡 Latin for ‘white tablet’ 💿 Photo albums started as blank white pages.

⚠ Odd plural: die Alben

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Lots of people in Germany sing in a choir (Chor). Churches, companies and local clubs (Vereine) often have one, and people of all ages join just for fun.

At parties and festivals you'll often hear Schlager, a kind of very German pop. People love to tease it as cheesy — and then everyone sings along the moment it comes on.

If you practise an instrument at home, check your building's Hausordnung. Many buildings have quiet hours (Ruhezeiten), often at lunchtime and at night. 🎹

✅ Quick quiz

Q. A friend asks about a singer. You love how she sounds. What do you say?

  1. ① Sie hat einen tollen Rhythmus.
  2. ② Das Lied ist ein Hit.
  3. ③ Sie hat eine tolle Stimme.
Show the answer

③ Sie hat eine tolle Stimme.
die Stimme = voice, so Sie hat eine tolle Stimme. means she has a great voice.
