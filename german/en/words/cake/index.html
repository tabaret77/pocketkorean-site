Today's words · 🍰 Cakes & desserts

10 sweet German words for cake lovers

A1A2B1

No more pointing at the cake counter and hoping for the best.

Three o'clock on a Sunday, and the café counter is full of cakes. You know exactly what you want, but not what it's called, so you keep saying „Das da, bitte“ — and then miss the question about cream.

Today's ten words are all about cakes and desserts. With them you can order in a café, choose your ice cream flavour and pay a proper compliment at a friend's Kaffee und Kuchen. 🍰

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. der Kuchen — cake
  2. die Vanille — vanilla
  3. der Zucker — sugar
  4. das Eis — ice cream; ice
  5. der Nachtisch — dessert
  6. die Schokolade — chocolate
  7. der Keks — biscuit, cookie
  8. der Pudding — pudding (custard-like dessert)
  9. die Schlagsahne — whipped cream; whipping cream
  10. das Gebäck — pastries, biscuits, baked goods

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

der Kuchen

[KOO-khen]cake

(Pl. die Kuchen)

📌 Möchtest du ein Stück Kuchen?

→ Would you like a piece of cake?

💡 Same Germanic root as cake 🎂 Say it with a happy throaty ch.

⚠ The plural is the same: die Kuchen.

02

die Vanille

[va-NIL-yuh]vanilla

(no pl.)

📌 Ich nehme eine Kugel Vanille, bitte.

→ One scoop of vanilla, please.

💡 From Spanish for ‘little pod’ 🌼 Vanilla grows in pods, after all.

Bonus At the ice cream shop: Eine Kugel Vanille, bitte.

03

der Zucker

[TSOOK-er]sugar

((pl. rare))

📌 Trinkst du den Kaffee mit Zucker?

→ Do you take sugar in your coffee?

💡 From Arabic sukkar — a cousin of sugar 🍬

Bonus sugar-free = ohne Zucker · icing sugar = der Puderzucker

04

das Eis

[ICE]ice cream; ice

((pl. rare) · Swiss: die/das Glace · Swiss: die/das Glacé)

📌 Im Sommer essen wir viel Eis.

→ In summer we eat a lot of ice cream.

💡 Looks like ice, but in a café it means ice cream 🍦 Summer's best word.

⚠ Ice cubes are also Eis, so context decides.

05

der Nachtisch

[NAKH-tish]dessert

(Pl. die Nachtische)

📌 Was gibt es zum Nachtisch?

→ What's for dessert?

💡 nach (after) + Tisch (table) = what comes after the meal 🍮 Dessert!

Bonus Same meaning: das Dessert · zum Nachtisch = for dessert

06

die Schokolade

[sho-ko-LAH-duh]chocolate

(Pl. die Schokoladen)

📌 Ich esse jeden Tag ein Stück Schokolade.

→ I eat a piece of chocolate every day.

💡 Ends in -ade, like lemonade — sweet stuff 🍫

Bonus Hot chocolate is eine heiße Schokolade.

07

der Keks

[KAYKS]biscuit, cookie

(Pl. die Kekse)

📌 Zum Tee gibt es Kekse.

→ There are biscuits with the tea.

💡 English cakes travelled to Germany and became Keks 🍪

Bonus Du gehst mir auf den Keks! = You're getting on my nerves!

08

der Pudding

[POOD-ing]pudding (custard-like dessert)

(Pl. die Puddings)

📌 Zum Nachtisch gibt es Schokoladenpudding.

→ There's chocolate pudding for dessert.

💡 Wobbly and borrowed straight from English 🍮

⚠ German Pudding is a set, custard-style dessert, not a British steamed pudding.

09

die Schlagsahne

[SHLAHK-zah-nuh]whipped cream; whipping cream

((no pl.) · Swiss: der Schlagrahm · Austrian: das/der Schlagobers)

📌 Möchten Sie den Kuchen mit Schlagsahne?

→ Would you like your cake with whipped cream?

💡 schlagen (beat) + Sahne (cream) = cream you beat up 🥄 Arm workout included.

Bonus In Austria: das Schlagobers

10

das Gebäck

[guh-BECK]pastries, biscuits, baked goods

(Sg.)

📌 Zum Kaffee gibt es selbst gemachtes Gebäck.

→ Home-made biscuits are served with the coffee.

💡 From backen (bake): everything baked, bundled into one word 🥐

⚠ Usually singular only: das Gebäck

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Germany has a lovely afternoon ritual called Kaffee und Kuchen. Especially at weekends, families and friends often get together around three or four o'clock for coffee and homemade cake.

A Kuchen is usually a simpler cake, like apple cake, while the tall, layered cream cakes are called Torte. A shop that makes them, often with a café, is a Konditorei.

And if it's your birthday at work, it's often you who brings the cake for your colleagues. It surprises lots of newcomers! 🎂

✅ Quick quiz

Q. In a café, you want a slice of cake with whipped cream. What do you say?

  1. ① Ein Stück Kuchen mit Keks, bitte.
  2. ② Einen Nachtisch mit Zucker, bitte.
  3. ③ Ein Stück Kuchen mit Schlagsahne, bitte.
Show the answer

③ Ein Stück Kuchen mit Schlagsahne, bitte.
Schlagsahne is whipped cream, so this orders cake with cream on top.

At snack time today, order out loud: „Ein Stück Kuchen mit Schlagsahne, bitte!“ The diet can start tomorrow! 🍰
