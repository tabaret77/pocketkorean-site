Today's words · 🏞️ Landscapes

Mountains, rivers, islands: 10 German landscape words

A2B1B2

Next time you watch Germany roll past a train window, you'll be able to name it all.

You're on the train from Berlin to Munich. First it's endless flat fields, then a river, then hills, and finally white mountains in the distance. You want to write a postcard, but all you've got is ‘mountain’ and ‘river’.

Today's ten words describe landscapes. With them you can plan a trip, describe where you've been and read the signs on a hiking trail. 🏞️

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. die Landschaft — landscape, scenery
  2. der Berg — mountain
  3. der Fluss — river
  4. das Gebirge — mountains, mountain range
  5. das Ufer — bank, shore
  6. das Tal — valley
  7. die Küste — coast
  8. die Insel — island
  9. der Hügel — hill
  10. der Wasserfall — waterfall

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

die Landschaft

[LANT-shaft]landscape, scenery

(Pl. die Landschaften)

📌 Die Landschaft in Bayern ist wunderschön.

→ The scenery in Bavaria is beautiful.

💡 Land + -schaft = the shape of the land, twin of English landscape 🖼️

Bonus Nouns ending in -schaft are always die.

02

der Berg

[BAIRK]mountain

(Pl. die Berge)

📌 Wir wandern auf einen Berg.

→ We're hiking up a mountain.

💡 Heidelberg, Nuremberg (Nürnberg)… the -berg in place names means mountain ⛰️

Bonus auf einen Berg wandern = to hike up a mountain

03

der Fluss

[FLOOSS]river

(Pl. die Flüsse)

📌 Die Spree ist ein Fluss in Berlin.

→ The Spree is a river in Berlin.

💡 From fließen (to flow) 🌊 Related to English flow and flux.

⚠ Umlaut in the plural: die Flüsse

04

das Gebirge

[guh-BEER-guh]mountains, mountain range

(Pl. die Gebirge)

📌 Im Winter fahren wir zum Skifahren ins Gebirge.

→ In winter we go skiing in the mountains.

💡 Ge- (a group) + Berg = a whole gang of mountains 🏔️

Bonus ins Gebirge fahren = to go to the mountains

05

das Ufer

[OO-fer]bank, shore

(Pl. die Ufer)

📌 Wir machen ein Picknick am Ufer.

→ We're having a picnic on the riverbank.

💡 Where the water says ‘oof’ and hits the land 🏖️ River bank or lake shore.

Bonus am Ufer = on the bank, by the water

06

das Tal

[TAHL]valley

(Pl. die Täler)

📌 Unten im Tal liegt ein kleines Dorf.

→ Down in the valley there is a small village.

💡 Same root as English dale 🏡 Between two Berge there's a Tal.

⚠ Umlaut in the plural: die Täler

07

die Küste

[KÜS-tuh]coast

(Pl. die Küsten)

📌 Wir machen Urlaub an der Küste.

→ We're spending our holiday on the coast.

💡 Looks like coast — both come from Latin costa, side 🌅

Bonus an der Küste = on the coast · Germany has the North Sea and the Baltic.

08

die Insel

[IN-zel]island

(Pl. die Inseln)

📌 Rügen ist die größte Insel Deutschlands.

→ Rügen is Germany's largest island.

💡 From Latin insula 🏝️ Think English insular, or peninsula.

Bonus A peninsula is die Halbinsel = half-island.

09

der Hügel

[HÜ-gel]hill

(Pl. die Hügel)

📌 Hinter dem Dorf liegt ein kleiner Hügel.

→ There's a small hill behind the village.

💡 Maybe related to hoch (high) 🌄 Higher than flat, smaller than a Berg.

Bonus hügelig = hilly

10

der Wasserfall

[VASS-er-fal]waterfall

(Pl. die Wasserfälle)

📌 Der Wasserfall stürzt aus fünfzig Metern Höhe in die Tiefe.

→ The waterfall plunges from a height of fifty metres.

💡 Wasser + Fall = waterfall, word for word 💦

⚠ Umlaut in the plural: die Wasserfälle

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Northern and southern Germany look very different. The north is flat, with coasts on the North Sea (Nordsee) and the Baltic (Ostsee). Heading south, you get more hills and forests, until the Alps appear in Bavaria. Germany's highest mountain is the Zugspitze.

Germans love hiking (Wandern). At weekends lots of people put on their boots and head for the forest or the mountains — and a break at a mountain hut (Hütte) is half the fun.

Rivers matter too. Along the Rhine valley you'll find castles on the hills and vineyards on the slopes, and many visitors enjoy it from a boat. 🏰

✅ Quick quiz

Q. You're on holiday by the sea. How do you tell a friend where you are?

  1. ① Wir sind im Gebirge.
  2. ② Wir sind an der Küste.
  3. ③ Wir sind im Tal.
Show the answer

② Wir sind an der Küste.
die Küste = the coast, and ‘on the coast’ is an der Küste.

Think of your favourite place and describe it out loud: ‘Dort gibt es einen Fluss, Berge und …’ ⛰️
