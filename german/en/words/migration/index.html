Today's words · 🌏 Migration & integration

Understand the news: 10 German words on migration and integration

B1B2

Live in Germany for a while and you'll meet these words everywhere — on the news, at the authorities, even in your own paperwork.

Switch on the German evening news and words like Migration and Integration come up again and again. It's the same when you renew your visa or read a notice from your language school. And if you're a foreigner living in Germany, it's not just someone else's story.

Today's ten words are about migration and integration. With them you can follow news headlines and talk in German about things that may touch your own life, like integration courses or naturalisation. 🌏

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. die Migration — migration
  2. der Einwanderer — immigrant
  3. einwandern — to immigrate
  4. der Flüchtling — refugee
  5. das Asyl — asylum
  6. die Integration — integration
  7. der Integrationskurs — integration course (German language and orientation course for immigrants)
  8. die Staatsbürgerschaft — citizenship
  9. die Einbürgerung — naturalization
  10. der Migrationshintergrund — migrant background, immigrant background

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

die Migration

[mee-gra-TSYOHN]migration

(Pl. die Migrationen)

📌 Migration ist ein wichtiges Thema in der Politik.

→ Migration is an important issue in politics.

💡 Nouns ending in -tion are always die 🌍 Same as English migration.

Bonus a migrant = der Migrant / die Migrantin

02

der Einwanderer

[INE-van-der-er]immigrant

(Pl. die Einwanderer (f: die Einwanderin))

📌 Viele Einwanderer gründen in Deutschland eigene Firmen.

→ Many immigrants set up their own companies in Germany.

💡 ein (in) + wandern (to wander) + -er (person) = someone who wanders in 🧳

Bonus Someone who leaves is der Auswanderer · female: die Einwanderin

03

einwandern

[INE-van-dern]to immigrate

(wandert ein – ist eingewandert)

📌 Seine Großeltern sind in den 60er-Jahren aus Italien eingewandert.

→ His grandparents immigrated from Italy in the 60s.

💡 To wander (wandern) in (ein) 🚶 Same verb as wandern, to hike.

Bonus The opposite: auswandern = to emigrate

⚠ Separable, and the perfect uses sein: Sie ist 2015 eingewandert.

04

der Flüchtling

[FLUEKHT-ling]refugee

(Pl. die Flüchtlinge)

📌 Deutschland hat viele Flüchtlinge aufgenommen.

→ Germany has taken in many refugees.

💡 die Flucht (flight, fleeing) + -ling (person) 🕊️ Nouns ending in -ling are der.

Bonus The media also often say die Geflüchteten (people who have fled).

05

das Asyl

[ah-ZEWL]asylum

(Sg.)

📌 Die Familie hat in Deutschland Asyl beantragt.

→ The family applied for asylum in Germany.

💡 From Greek asylon, ‘a place that can't be plundered’ 🛡️ Same root as asylum.

Bonus to apply for asylum = Asyl beantragen

06

die Integration

[in-te-gra-TSYOHN]integration

(Pl. die Integrationen)

📌 Der Integrationskurs hilft bei der Integration.

→ The integration course helps with integration.

💡 From Latin integrare, ‘to make whole’ 🧩 -tion, so die again!

Bonus The verb: sich integrieren = to integrate, settle in

07

der Integrationskurs

[in-te-gra-TSYOHNS-koors]integration course (German language and orientation course for immigrants)

(Pl. die Integrationskurse)

📌 Im Integrationskurs lernt man Deutsch und etwas über Politik und Geschichte.

→ In the integration course you learn German and something about politics and history.

💡 Integration + s + Kurs 📚 It ends in der Kurs, so it's der.

Bonus After the language part comes der Orientierungskurs on everyday life, history and society.

08

die Staatsbürgerschaft

[SHTAHTS-bewr-ger-shaft]citizenship

(Pl. die Staatsbürgerschaften)

📌 Nach acht Jahren beantragte er die deutsche Staatsbürgerschaft.

→ After eight years he applied for German citizenship.

💡 Staat (state) + Bürger (citizen) + -schaft 🏛️ Words ending in -schaft are always die.

Bonus On official forms you'll more often see die Staatsangehörigkeit.

09

die Einbürgerung

[INE-bewr-ger-oong]naturalization

(Pl. die Einbürgerungen)

📌 Für die Einbürgerung muss man einen Test bestehen.

→ You have to pass a test to be naturalized.

💡 Becoming one of the Bürger 🏰 A Bürger was originally someone living inside the Burg (castle).

Bonus citizenship test = der Einbürgerungstest

10

der Migrationshintergrund

[mee-gra-TSYOHNS-hin-ter-groont]migrant background, immigrant background

(Sg.)

📌 Ein Viertel der Bevölkerung hat einen Migrationshintergrund.

→ A quarter of the population has a migrant background.

💡 hinter (behind) + Grund (ground) = background 🌏 Just like the English word, piece by piece.

Bonus Menschen mit Migrationshintergrund = people with a migrant background

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Germany has been a country of immigration for a long time. From the 1950s to the 1970s, many ‘Gastarbeiter’ (guest workers) came from countries such as Italy, Greece and Turkey, and their children and grandchildren are now an important part of German society.

Integration courses (Integrationskurs) teach German along with everyday life, history and politics in Germany. Who can take part and what support is available depends on your residence situation, so it's best to ask your local foreigners' office (Ausländerbehörde).

Some people now prefer expressions like ‘Einwanderungsgeschichte’ (immigration history) instead of Migrationshintergrund. It's a topic where word choice matters. 🤝

Q. A friend is talking about their parents. Fill the gap: ‘Meine Eltern ___ 1990 nach Deutschland eingewandert.’

  1. ① sind
  2. ② haben
  3. ③ werden
Show the answer

① sind
einwandern describes moving from one place to another, so the perfect uses sein: sind eingewandert.
