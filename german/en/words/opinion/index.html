Today's words · 💭 Giving opinions

Speak your mind: 10 German words for opinions

A1A2B1

In Germany you'll be asked ‘What do you think?’ far more often than you might expect.

In a work meeting my manager suddenly turned to me: ‘Was meinst du dazu?’ I had an opinion, but no idea how to start the sentence, so I said ‘Erm… gut?’ and that was it. I replayed that moment all evening.

Today's ten little words are for giving your opinion, agreeing and disagreeing. With them you can say what you think clearly — in meetings and over coffee with friends. 💬

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. die Meinung — opinion
  2. meinen — to think, to believe; to mean
  3. finden — to find; to think, consider
  4. glauben — to believe; to think
  5. meiner Meinung nach — in my opinion
  6. einverstanden — in agreement, agreed
  7. recht haben — to be right
  8. stimmen — to be right, to be correct; to vote
  9. zustimmen — to agree (with) (+ dat.)
  10. dagegen — against it; into it; on the other hand

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

die Meinung

[MY-noong]opinion

(Pl. die Meinungen)

📌 Meiner Meinung nach ist das eine gute Idee.

→ In my opinion that's a good idea.

💡 meinen (to think) + -ung = what you think 💭 -ung, so die Meinung!

Bonus Ich bin anderer Meinung. = I see it differently.

02

meinen

[MY-nen]to think, to believe; to mean

(meint – hat gemeint)

📌 Was meinst du dazu?

→ What do you think about it?

💡 Same root as English mean 🤔 It means both ‘to think’ and ‘to mean’.

Bonus Was meinst du? = What do you think? · So habe ich das nicht gemeint. = That's not what I meant.

03

finden

[FIN-den]to find; to think, consider

(findet – hat gefunden)

📌 Wie findest du das Buch?

→ What do you think of the book?

💡 Just like English find 🔍 And like English, you can ‘find’ something good or bad.

Bonus Wie findest du das? = What do you think of it? · Ich finde das super!

04

glauben

[GLOW-ben]to believe; to think

(glaubt – hat geglaubt)

📌 Ich glaube, es regnet morgen.

→ I think it'll rain tomorrow.

💡 The ‘lieve’ in English believe is a cousin of glauben 🙏

Bonus Believing a person takes the dative: Ich glaube dir. · believe in = an + acc.

05

meiner Meinung nach

[MY-ner MY-noong nahkh]in my opinion

📌 Meiner Meinung nach ist das Wetter heute zu schlecht für einen Ausflug.

→ In my opinion the weather today is too bad for an outing.

💡 Here nach comes at the end 🔚 ‘According to my opinion’.

⚠ The verb comes straight after: Meiner Meinung nach ist das falsch.

06

einverstanden

[INE-fair-shtahn-den]in agreement, agreed

(= einverstanden sein)

📌 Bist du damit einverstanden?

→ Do you agree with that?

💡 ein (one) + verstanden (understood) = understood as one 🤝 German for OK, deal!

Bonus agree with something = mit + dat.: Ich bin damit einverstanden.

07

recht haben

[REKHT hah-ben]to be right

(hat recht – hatte recht)

📌 Du hast recht, es ist zu spät.

→ You're right, it's too late.

💡 German says you ‘have right’ ✅ Du hast recht!

⚠ haben, not sein! Du bist recht (wrong) → Du hast recht (right)

08

stimmen

[SHTIM-en]to be right, to be correct; to vote

(stimmt – hat gestimmt)

📌 Das stimmt!

→ That's right!

💡 From die Stimme (voice, vote) 🗳️ To agree in one word: Stimmt!

Bonus When paying, Stimmt so! = Keep the change.

09

zustimmen

[TSOO-shtim-en]to agree (with) (+ dat.)

(stimmt zu – hat zugestimmt)

📌 Da stimme ich dir zu.

→ I agree with you on that.

💡 zu (towards) + stimmen = giving your vote to someone 👍

⚠ Separable and with the dative: Da stimme ich dir zu.

10

dagegen

[dah-GAY-gen]against it; into it; on the other hand

📌 Hast du etwas dagegen?

→ Do you have any objection?

💡 da (that) + gegen (against) = against it 🙅‍♀️ In favour is dafür!

Bonus Hast du was dagegen? = Do you mind?

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

In Germany, sharing your own opinion in meetings, classes and conversations is valued. Staying quiet can be taken as agreement, so even a short Meiner Meinung nach… helps.

There's a fun word for when you're torn: ‘Jein’, a mix of ja and nein. It means ‘yes and no’, and you'll hear it all the time.

When you disagree, giving a reason — ‘Das sehe ich anders, aber…’ — keeps things friendly. Many Germans genuinely enjoy a good Diskussion. 🗣️

✅ Quick quiz

Q. A friend asks: ‘Cinema on Friday at eight?’ You say yes.

  1. ① Hast du etwas dagegen?
  2. ② Ja, einverstanden!
  3. ③ Meiner Meinung nach nicht.
Show the answer

② Ja, einverstanden!
einverstanden means agreed — it's how you accept a suggestion.
