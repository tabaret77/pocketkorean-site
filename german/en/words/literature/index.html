Today's words · 📚 Literature & books

Talking books: 10 German words from the land of poets

A1A2B1B2

Next time someone in Germany asks what you're reading, you'll have a proper answer.

It's a rainy afternoon and you step into a little bookshop (Buchhandlung). The bookseller asks, ‘Suchen Sie etwas Bestimmtes?’ You want to ask for a good novel and chat about your favourite author — but is a novel a Roman or a Novelle?

Today's ten words are about books and literature. Use them to find a book in a shop, talk about the plot at a book club or survive the poem your German teacher wants you to learn by heart. 📚

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. die Literatur — literature; reference works, bibliography
  2. das Buch — book
  3. der Roman — novel
  4. das Gedicht — poem
  5. der Autor — author, writer
  6. der Schriftsteller — writer, author
  7. das Kapitel — chapter
  8. der Dichter — poet; writer
  9. die Erzählung — story, tale; short story
  10. veröffentlichen — to publish; to make public, to release

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

die Literatur

[lit-er-a-TOOR]literature; reference works, bibliography

(Pl. die Literaturen)

📌 Sie studiert deutsche Literatur.

→ She studies German literature.

💡 From Latin littera, letter ✒️ Letters add up to literature.

Bonus In academic writing it also means ‘reading list, references’.

02

das Buch

[BOOKH]book

(Pl. die Bücher)

📌 Ich lese gerade ein gutes Buch.

→ I'm reading a good book at the moment.

💡 People once wrote on beech (Buche) tablets 🌳 Buche → Buch → book.

⚠ Umlaut in the plural: die Bücher

03

der Roman

[ro-MAHN]novel

(Pl. die Romane)

📌 Im Urlaub lese ich einen dicken Roman.

→ On vacation I'm reading a thick novel.

💡 Not a romance — just a novel 📖 Stress the end: ro-MAHN.

⚠ A romance novel is ein Liebesroman.

04

das Gedicht

[guh-DIKHT]poem

(Pl. die Gedichte)

📌 Wir müssen ein Gedicht auswendig lernen.

→ We have to learn a poem by heart.

💡 From dichten, to write poetry 🌙 Lots of ge- nouns are das.

Bonus ein Gedicht auswendig lernen = to learn a poem by heart

05

der Autor

[OW-tor]author, writer

(Pl. die Autoren (f: die Autorin))

📌 Wer ist der Autor von diesem Buch?

→ Who's the author of this book?

💡 Not a car (Auto) — an author ✍️ Stress the first syllable.

Bonus A female author is die Autorin.

06

der Schriftsteller

[SHRIFT-shtel-er]writer, author

(Pl. die Schriftsteller (f: die Schriftstellerin))

📌 Mein Lieblingsschriftsteller schreibt Krimis.

→ My favorite writer writes crime novels.

💡 Schrift (writing) + stellen (to place) + -er = someone who puts writing in place 🖋️

Bonus A female writer is die Schriftstellerin.

07

das Kapitel

[ka-PIT-el]chapter

(Pl. die Kapitel)

📌 Lies bitte bis morgen das zweite Kapitel.

→ Please read the second chapter by tomorrow.

💡 Latin for ‘little head’, the heading 🔖 A sibling of English chapter.

⚠ Gender trap: it's das Kapitel.

08

der Dichter

[DIKH-ter]poet; writer

(Pl. die Dichter (f: die Dichterin))

📌 Goethe und Schiller gelten als die größten deutschen Dichter.

→ Goethe and Schiller are regarded as the greatest German poets.

💡 dichten (to write poetry) + -er = poet 🪶 Goethe and Schiller are the famous pair.

Bonus Germany's nickname: das Land der Dichter und Denker

09

die Erzählung

[air-TSAY-loong]story, tale; short story

(Pl. die Erzählungen)

📌 Die Erzählung spielt in einem kleinen Dorf.

→ The story is set in a small village.

💡 erzählen (to tell) + -ung = a tale 🗣️ -ung means die, of course.

Bonus Erzähl mal! = Go on, tell me!

10

veröffentlichen

[fair-ERF-ent-likh-en]to publish; to make public, to release

(veröffentlicht – hat veröffentlicht)

📌 Sie hat ihr erstes Buch veröffentlicht.

→ She has published her first book.

💡 öffentlich (public) is hiding inside 📢 Make it public = publish.

Bonus The noun: die Veröffentlichung = publication

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Germany likes to call itself the ‘land of poets and thinkers’ (Land der Dichter und Denker). Goethe, Schiller, Thomas Mann and Kafka are school reading, and almost every German student has carried around one of the small yellow Reclam paperbacks.

Every October the Frankfurt Book Fair (Frankfurter Buchmesse) takes place, and Leipzig holds its book fair in spring. Small local bookshops are still common, and asking the bookseller for a recommendation is very normal.

Also, new German-language books generally cost the same everywhere thanks to fixed book prices, whether you buy online or round the corner. So you can support your local bookshop with a clear conscience. 📖

✅ Quick quiz

Q. In a bookshop, you want to say you're looking for a thick novel for your holiday.

  1. ① Ich suche einen dicken Roman.
  2. ② Ich suche ein kurzes Gedicht.
  3. ③ Ich suche ein neues Kapitel.
Show the answer

① Ich suche einen dicken Roman.
der Roman = novel, so a thick novel is ein dicker Roman.

Think of the book you're reading now and say out loud: ‘Ich lese gerade … von …’ — with the author's name! 📚
