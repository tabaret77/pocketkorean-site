Today's words · 🏛️ Architecture

Look up! 10 German words for buildings

A2B1B2

The moment you look up at the buildings, a German city starts to look completely different.

You step out of Cologne's main station and a huge cathedral fills the sky. An older lady next to you says proudly, "An dem Dom hat man über 600 Jahre gebaut!" — and all you can manage is "Schön!"

Today's ten words are about architecture and buildings. With them you can follow a city tour and say a sentence or two about a building you like. 🏛️

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. das Gebäude — building
  2. die Architektur — architecture
  3. der Architekt — architect
  4. bauen — to build, to construct
  5. der Turm — tower
  6. die Brücke — bridge
  7. der Stil — style
  8. das Hochhaus — high-rise (building), tower block
  9. der Dom — cathedral
  10. die Säule — column, pillar

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

das Gebäude

[guh-BOY-duh]building

(Pl. die Gebäude)

📌 Das Gebäude ist über 100 Jahre alt.

→ The building is over 100 years old.

💡 A noun from bauen (to build) 🏢 Something built = a building.

Bonus The plural looks the same: ein Gebäude, zwei Gebäude

02

die Architektur

[ar-khi-tek-TOOR]architecture

(Pl. die Architekturen)

📌 Ich interessiere mich sehr für moderne Architektur.

→ I'm very interested in modern architecture.

💡 A borrowed noun in -ur, so die 📐 Almost the same as English.

Bonus Adjective: architektonisch = architectural

03

der Architekt

[ar-khi-TEKT]architect

(Pl. die Architekten (f: die Architektin))

📌 Der Architekt zeigt uns die Pläne für das Haus.

→ The architect shows us the plans for the house.

💡 Greek for ‘master builder’ 👷 Just like English architect.

⚠ Weak noun: den/dem/des Architekten

04

bauen

[BOW-en]to build, to construct

(baut – hat gebaut)

📌 Hier wird ein neues Haus gebaut.

→ A new house is being built here.

💡 Stack it up like Lego 🧱 Houses, bridges, snowmen — you bauen them all.

Bonus building site = die Baustelle (you'll see them everywhere)

05

der Turm

[toorm]tower

(Pl. die Türme)

📌 Vom Turm hat man eine tolle Aussicht.

→ There's a great view from the tower.

💡 From Latin turris 🗼 English turret is a cousin.

⚠ Plural with umlaut: die Türme

06

die Brücke

[BRÜ-kuh]bridge

(Pl. die Brücken)

📌 Wir gehen über die Brücke.

→ We walk across the bridge.

💡 Same root as English bridge 🌉 The g just turned into ck.

Bonus A ‘bridge day’ between a holiday and the weekend = der Brückentag

07

der Stil

[shteel]style

(Pl. die Stile)

📌 Ihr Kleid hat einen eleganten Stil.

→ Her dress has an elegant style.

💡 From Latin stilus (pen) 🖋️ A way of writing → a style.

⚠ Sounds exactly like der Stiel (handle, stem).

08

das Hochhaus

[HOHKH-hows]high-rise (building), tower block

(Pl. die Hochhäuser)

📌 Wir wohnen im zehnten Stock eines Hochhauses.

→ We live on the tenth floor of a high-rise.

💡 hoch (high) + Haus (house) = a high house 🏙️ Frankfurt is full of them.

⚠ Plural with umlaut and -er: die Hochhäuser

09

der Dom

[dohm]cathedral

(Pl. die Dome)

📌 Der Kölner Dom ist sehr berühmt.

→ Cologne Cathedral is very famous.

💡 From Latin domus (house) = the house of the church ⛪ der Kölner Dom!

⚠ Not a dome! A dome is die Kuppel.

10

die Säule

[ZOY-luh]column, pillar

(Pl. die Säulen)

📌 Das Dach ruht auf vier Säulen.

→ The roof rests on four columns.

💡 Säule sounds like ‘soy-luh’ — picture a giant soy sauce bottle holding up the roof 🏛️

Bonus Figuratively too: eine Säule der Gesellschaft = a pillar of society

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

In old town centres you'll often see a Fachwerkhaus, a half-timbered house with its wooden frame on show. They look like something out of a fairy tale.

The Bauhaus, which shaped modern design worldwide, also began in Germany. It was a school founded in Weimar in 1919, famous for a simple, practical style.

Architecture words also pop up when you look for a flat. In ads, Altbau usually means an older building with high ceilings, and Neubau a more recently built one. 🏠

✅ Quick quiz

Q. In Cologne, a friend points at the cathedral and asks what it is. What do you say?

  1. ① Das ist ein Hochhaus.
  2. ② Das ist eine Brücke.
  3. ③ Das ist der Kölner Dom.
Show the answer

③ Das ist der Kölner Dom.
der Dom means cathedral, and Cologne's is der Kölner Dom.
