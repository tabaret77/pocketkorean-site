Today's words · 🌱 Environment

Sorting it out: 10 German words for the environment

A2B1B2

There's a reason German kitchens have four bins instead of one.

On your first day in a German flat, you find a whole row of bins in the kitchen. Where does the yoghurt pot go? The pizza box? Your flatmate grins: "Wir trennen hier den Müll!" Then you switch on the news and it's all Umwelt and nachhaltig.

Today's ten words come up in every conversation about the environment. With them you can follow the recycling rules, read the headlines and talk green with your friends. 🌱

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. die Umwelt — environment
  2. der Umweltschutz — environmental protection
  3. schützen — to protect (from)
  4. die Umweltverschmutzung — environmental pollution
  5. der Müll — rubbish, garbage, trash
  6. das Plastik — plastic
  7. recyceln — to recycle
  8. umweltfreundlich — environmentally friendly, eco-friendly
  9. nachhaltig — sustainable; lasting
  10. erneuerbar — renewable

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

die Umwelt

[OOM-velt]environment

(no pl.)

📌 Wir sollten mehr für die Umwelt tun.

→ We should do more for the environment.

💡 um (around) + Welt (world) = the world around you 🌍 That's your environment.

⚠ No plural — it's always die Umwelt.

02

der Umweltschutz

[OOM-velt-shoots]environmental protection

(Sg.)

📌 Umweltschutz fängt schon beim Mülltrennen an.

→ Protecting the environment starts with sorting your rubbish.

💡 Umwelt + Schutz (protection) 🛡️ Compounds take the gender of the last part, so it's der.

Bonus Same Schutz in der Datenschutz = data protection

03

schützen

[SHUET-sen]to protect (from)

(schützt (vor + dat.) – hat geschützt)

📌 Sonnencreme schützt die Haut vor Sonnenbrand.

→ Sun cream protects the skin from sunburn.

💡 der Schutz (protection) + -en = to protect 🧴 Sun cream schützt your skin.

⚠ Protect from = vor + dative: vor der Sonne schützen (not von)

04

die Umweltverschmutzung

[OOM-velt-fair-shmoot-soong]environmental pollution

(Sg.)

📌 Autos tragen stark zur Umweltverschmutzung bei.

→ Cars contribute heavily to pollution.

💡 Spot Schmutz (dirt) in the middle? 🏭 Making the environment dirty = pollution.

Bonus dirty = schmutzig · to pollute = verschmutzen

05

der Müll

[MUEL]rubbish, garbage, trash

(no pl.)

📌 Bitte bring den Müll runter.

→ Please take the rubbish down.

💡 Push your lips out for the ü and you'll pull the face of someone smelling rubbish 🗑️

Bonus bin = der Mülleimer · to sort rubbish = den Müll trennen

06

das Plastik

[PLAS-tik]plastic

(Sg.)

📌 Wir wollen weniger Plastik benutzen.

→ We want to use less plastic.

💡 Just like plastic 🥤 From Greek for ‘mouldable’.

⚠ das Plastik = plastic, die Plastik = a sculpture. Watch the article!

07

recyceln

[ree-SY-keln]to recycle

(recycelt – hat recycelt)

📌 Altpapier kann mehrmals recycelt werden.

→ Waste paper can be recycled several times.

💡 English recycle with a German -n stuck on ♻️ Say it the English way.

⚠ Mind the spelling: recyceln · no ge- in the perfect: recycelt

08

umweltfreundlich

[OOM-velt-froynt-likh]environmentally friendly, eco-friendly

(umweltfreundlicher – am umweltfreundlichsten)

📌 Fahrradfahren ist umweltfreundlicher als Autofahren.

→ Cycling is more environmentally friendly than driving.

💡 Umwelt + freundlich (friendly) = friendly to the environment 💚 Literally ‘eco-friendly’.

Bonus Opposite: umweltschädlich = harmful to the environment

09

nachhaltig

[NAKH-hal-tikh]sustainable; lasting

(nachhaltiger – am nachhaltigsten)

📌 Viele Firmen wollen nachhaltiger produzieren.

→ Many companies want to produce more sustainably.

💡 nach (after) + halten (to hold) = holding on long afterwards 🌳 It started in German forestry.

Bonus The noun: die Nachhaltigkeit = sustainability

10

erneuerbar

[air-NOY-er-bar]renewable

📌 Mehr als die Hälfte des Stroms kommt inzwischen aus erneuerbaren Energien.

→ More than half of our electricity now comes from renewable energy.

💡 er- + neu (new) + -bar (-able) = able to be made new again 🔋 -bar works like -able.

Bonus renewable energy = erneuerbare Energien (plural in German)

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Sorting rubbish (Mülltrennung) is part of everyday life in Germany. You'll usually split it into paper, packaging (Gelbe Tonne or Gelber Sack), food waste (Biomüll) and everything else (Restmüll). Bin colours and the finer rules vary from town to town, so check your building's notice when you move in.

Glass goes into street containers, sorted into white, green and brown. Signs often ask you not to use them at night or on Sundays because of the noise. Bottles with a deposit (Pfand) go back to the supermarket instead.

And the word nachhaltig comes from 18th-century German forestry: the idea of only cutting as many trees as can grow back. That idea became the root of ‘sustainability’ worldwide. 🌲

✅ Quick quiz

Q. How do you say "Cycling is more eco-friendly than driving"?

  1. ① Radfahren ist umweltschädlicher als Autofahren.
  2. ② Radfahren ist umweltfreundlicher als Autofahren.
  3. ③ Radfahren ist genauso schmutzig wie Autofahren.
Show the answer

② Radfahren ist umweltfreundlicher als Autofahren.
umweltfreundlich = eco-friendly, and the comparative is umweltfreundlicher; umweltschädlich means the opposite.
