Today's words · 🎸 Musical instruments

10 German words for music lovers

A1A2B1B2

In Germany, chatting about instruments is one of the fastest ways to make friends.

You visit a German friend: there's a piano in the living room and squeaky recorder practice coming from the kids' room. Your friend asks, “Spielst du ein Instrument?” You had piano lessons as a child — but how do you say that in German?

Today's ten words are about instruments, playing and practising. You'll be able to say what you play and how often you practise, naturally. 🎸

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. das Instrument — (musical) instrument; tool
  2. spielen — to play
  3. das Klavier — piano
  4. die Gitarre — guitar
  5. die Geige — violin, fiddle
  6. die Flöte — flute; recorder, pipe
  7. das Schlagzeug — drums, drum kit; percussion
  8. die Trompete — trumpet
  9. üben — to practice
  10. die Orgel — organ (instrument)

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

das Instrument

[in-stroo-MENT](musical) instrument; tool

(Pl. die Instrumente)

📌 Spielst du ein Instrument?

→ Do you play an instrument?

💡 Spelled exactly like English 🎻 Just put the stress on the end.

⚠ It's das in German. Plural: die Instrumente.

02

spielen

[SHPEE-len]to play

(spielt – hat gespielt)

📌 Die Kinder spielen gern Fußball.

→ The children like playing football.

💡 Like play: games, sports and instruments are all spielen 🎮

Bonus Fußball spielen, Klavier spielen, Karten spielen — one verb for all.

03

das Klavier

[kla-VEER]piano

(Pl. die Klaviere)

📌 Meine Tochter lernt Klavier.

→ My daughter is learning the piano.

💡 From Latin clavis (key) — a row of keys 🎹 Think of Bach's clavier.

⚠ No article when you play: Ich spiele Klavier.

04

die Gitarre

[gi-TAR-uh]guitar

(Pl. die Gitarren)

📌 Mein Bruder spielt Gitarre.

→ My brother plays the guitar.

💡 guitar plus an -e 🎸 Stress on the ‘TAR’!

05

die Geige

[GUY-guh]violin, fiddle

(Pl. die Geigen)

📌 Meine Tochter spielt seit fünf Jahren Geige.

→ My daughter has been playing the violin for five years.

💡 A guy playing the Geige 🎻 It's the everyday word for violin.

Bonus die erste Geige spielen = to call the shots

06

die Flöte

[FLUR-tuh]flute; recorder, pipe

(Pl. die Flöten)

📌 Meine Tochter lernt Flöte.

→ My daughter is learning the flute.

💡 Related to flute 🪈 Round your lips for the ö.

Bonus The school recorder is die Blockflöte.

07

das Schlagzeug

[SHLAHK-tsoyk]drums, drum kit; percussion

(Pl. die Schlagzeuge)

📌 Mein Bruder spielt Schlagzeug in einer Band.

→ My brother plays drums in a band.

💡 schlagen (to hit) + Zeug (gear) = stuff you hit 🥁

Bonus drummer = der Schlagzeuger · die Schlagzeugerin

08

die Trompete

[trom-PAY-tuh]trumpet

(Pl. die Trompeten)

📌 Er spielt Trompete in einer Band.

→ He plays trumpet in a band.

💡 Almost trumpet 🎺 Blow it loud: trom-PAY-tuh!

09

üben

[Ü-ben]to practice

(übt – hat geübt)

📌 Ich übe jeden Tag Klavier.

→ I practice the piano every day.

💡 Üben every day and your skills go über the top 📈

Bonus Übung macht den Meister. = Practice makes perfect.

10

die Orgel

[OR-gel]organ (instrument)

(Pl. die Orgeln)

📌 In der Kirche spielt jemand Orgel.

→ Someone is playing the organ in the church.

💡 From Greek organon (tool), like English organ ⛪

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Many children in Germany learn the recorder (Blockflöte) at primary school. Most areas also have a music school (Musikschule) where children and adults take lessons.

Be careful when practising in a flat. Many buildings have quiet times (Ruhezeit), for example at night, around midday or on Sundays. The details depend on your building's house rules (Hausordnung), so check first.

This is Bach's country, and churches often host organ concerts. Many are free or ask only for a donation, so keep an eye on the noticeboard outside. 🎶

✅ Quick quiz

Q. A friend asks “Spielst du ein Instrument?” You play the piano. What do you say?

  1. ① Ja, ich spiele Klavier.
  2. ② Ja, ich höre ein Klavier.
  3. ③ Ja, ich gehe Klavier.
Show the answer

① Ja, ich spiele Klavier.
To play an instrument is spielen + the instrument with no article: Ich spiele Klavier.

Say what you play — or want to learn — with “Ich spiele …” or “Ich möchte Geige lernen.” 🎵
