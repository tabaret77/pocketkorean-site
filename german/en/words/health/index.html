Today's words · 💪 Health

Healthy body, healthy mind: 10 German words

A1A2B1B2

That word Germans say when you sneeze? It actually means ‘health’.

Every January my German friends make the same resolution: “Ich will gesünder leben!” Move more, eat well, sleep properly. But when I tried to join in, I didn't have the words.

Today's ten words are all about a healthy lifestyle. With them you can talk about food, exercise, sleep and stress in German. 💪

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. gesund — healthy
  2. die Gesundheit — health
  3. die Ernährung — diet, nutrition
  4. die Bewegung — movement; exercise
  5. der Schlaf — sleep
  6. der Stress — stress
  7. das Gewicht — weight
  8. abnehmen — to decrease; to lose weight; to take (off)
  9. entspannen — to relax
  10. die Vorbeugung — prevention

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

gesund

[guh-ZOONT]healthy

(gesund – gesünder – am gesündesten)

📌 Obst ist gesund.

→ Fruit is healthy.

💡 Same root as English sound, as in safe and sound 🍎 Obst ist gesund!

Bonus Umlaut in the comparative: gesund – gesünder – am gesündesten

02

die Gesundheit

[guh-ZOONT-hite]health

(no pl.)

📌 Sport ist gut für die Gesundheit.

→ Sport is good for your health.

💡 gesund + -heit = health 🤧 -heit nouns are always die — and it's what you say after a sneeze!

Bonus The person who sneezed just answers “Danke!”

03

die Ernährung

[air-NAIR-oong]diet, nutrition

(no pl.)

📌 Eine gesunde Ernährung ist wichtig für den Körper.

→ A healthy diet is important for the body.

💡 nähren (to nourish) is inside 🥗 -ung, so die.

Bonus eine gesunde Ernährung = a healthy diet

04

die Bewegung

[buh-VAY-goong]movement; exercise

(Pl. die Bewegungen)

📌 Bewegung ist gut für die Gesundheit.

→ Exercise is good for your health.

💡 bewegen (to move) + -ung = movement 🚶 Any activity counts, not just the gym.

Bonus Ich brauche mehr Bewegung. = I need to move more.

05

der Schlaf

[SHLAHF]sleep

(Sg.)

📌 Ich brauche mindestens acht Stunden Schlaf.

→ I need at least eight hours of sleep.

💡 Looks a lot like sleep, doesn't it? 😴 Drop the -en from schlafen and you have the noun.

Bonus Schlaf gut! = Sleep well!

06

der Stress

[SHTRESS]stress

((no pl.))

📌 Ich habe gerade viel Stress.

→ I'm under a lot of stress at the moment.

💡 English stress, but say the st as ‘sht’ 😵

Bonus Ich habe Stress mit meinem Chef. = I'm having trouble with my boss.

07

das Gewicht

[guh-VIKHT]weight

(Pl. die Gewichte)

📌 Ich achte auf mein Gewicht.

→ I watch my weight.

💡 From wiegen (to weigh) ⚖️ A cousin of English weight.

Bonus auf sein Gewicht achten = to watch your weight

08

abnehmen

[AP-nay-men]to decrease; to lose weight; to take (off)

(nimmt ab – hat abgenommen)

📌 Ich habe drei Kilo abgenommen.

→ I've lost three kilos.

💡 ab- (off) + nehmen (to take) = take the kilos off 📉

⚠ Separable: Ich nehme ab. The opposite is zunehmen (to gain weight).

09

entspannen

[ent-SHPAN-en]to relax

(entspannt – hat entspannt)

📌 Am Wochenende will ich nur entspannen.

→ At the weekend I just want to relax.

💡 ent- (un-) + spannen (to tighten) = untighten, relax 🧘

Bonus Often reflexive: Entspann dich! = Relax!

10

die Vorbeugung

[FOR-boy-goong]prevention

(Sg.)

📌 Regelmäßige Bewegung dient der Vorbeugung von Herzkrankheiten.

→ Regular exercise helps prevent heart disease.

💡 vor (before) + beugen (to bend) = head it off in advance 🛡️ Stop illness before it starts.

Bonus You'll also see die Prävention.

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Germans love a walk (Spaziergang), especially on Sunday afternoons in a park or forest. Even when it's grey, heading out for some “frische Luft” is completely normal.

Fresh air matters indoors too. Many people open their windows wide several times a day to air the room (Lüften), even briefly in winter.

Saunas are another popular habit. Many German saunas are mixed and swimwear-free, so if it's your first time, check the rules beforehand. 🧖
