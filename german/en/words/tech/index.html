AI, robots and algorithms: 10 German tech words

B1B2C1

There's hardly a day without ‘KI’ in German headlines — so let's start with what it means.

You open a German news app on the train and every headline is full of KI, Digitalisierung, Algorithmus… They look almost like English, but not quite — and you end up reaching for a translator.

Today's ten words come up again and again in news about AI and technology. They're long, but many look like English, so they're easier than they seem. With them, you can read tech headlines on your own! 🤖

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. die Künstliche Intelligenz — artificial intelligence (AI)
  2. der Roboter — robot
  3. die Technologie — technology
  4. digital — digital
  5. entwickeln — to develop
  6. die Digitalisierung — digitalization, digitization
  7. der Algorithmus — algorithm
  8. die Automatisierung — automation
  9. die Innovation — innovation
  10. das maschinelle Lernen — machine learning

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

die Künstliche Intelligenz

[KÜNST-likh-uh in-tel-ee-GENTS]artificial intelligence (AI)

(Sg.)

📌 Künstliche Intelligenz wird immer häufiger in der Medizin eingesetzt.

→ Artificial intelligence is being used more and more in medicine.

💡 Intelligence made by Kunst (skill, art) = artificial intelligence 🧠 Short form: KI, said ‘kah-EE’.

⚠ Germans don't say AI — they say KI.

02

der Roboter

[ROH-boh-ter]robot

(Pl. die Roboter)

📌 In der Autoindustrie arbeiten schon viele Roboter.

→ Many robots already work in the car industry.

💡 From Czech robota (hard labour) 🦾 A machine made to work!

Bonus Same in the plural: die Roboter · robot vacuum = der Saugroboter

03

die Technologie

[tekh-noh-loh-GEE]technology

(Pl. die Technologien)

📌 Neue Technologien verändern unser Leben.

→ New technologies are changing our lives.

💡 Ends in -ie, so it's die 💡 Stress the last syllable.

Bonus Similar: die Technik = technology, engineering, gadgets

04

digital

[dee-gee-TAHL]digital

📌 Heute bezahlen viele Leute digital.

→ Nowadays many people pay digitally.

💡 From Latin digitus (finger) 👆 Counting on your fingers became ‘digital’.

Bonus The opposite is analog.

05

entwickeln

[ent-VIK-eln]to develop

(entwickelt – hat entwickelt)

📌 Die Firma entwickelt eine neue App für Senioren.

→ The company is developing a new app for older people.

💡 ent- (un-) + wickeln (to wind) = to unwind, unfold 🧶 You unfold an idea into a product.

Bonus die Entwicklung = development · a developer = der Entwickler

06

die Digitalisierung

[dee-gee-tah-lee-ZEE-roong]digitalization, digitization

((no pl.))

📌 Die Digitalisierung verändert die Arbeitswelt.

→ Digitalization is changing the world of work.

💡 digital + -isierung (-ization) = digitalisation 📲 Ends in -ung, so it's die.

Bonus The verb: digitalisieren

07

der Algorithmus

[al-go-RIT-mooss]algorithm

(Pl. die Algorithmen)

📌 Der Algorithmus entscheidet, welche Beiträge wir sehen.

→ The algorithm decides which posts we see.

💡 Named after the Persian mathematician al-Khwarizmi 🧮 A person became a word!

⚠ Plural: die Algorithmen (-us becomes -en)

08

die Automatisierung

[ow-toh-mah-tee-ZEE-roong]automation

(Pl. die Automatisierungen)

📌 Durch die Automatisierung fallen viele einfache Jobs weg.

→ Automation is eliminating many simple jobs.

💡 automatisch + -isierung = automation ⚙️ Same pattern as Digitalisierung.

Bonus automatisch = automatic

09

die Innovation

[in-oh-vah-TSYOHN]innovation

(Pl. die Innovationen)

📌 Ohne Innovationen verliert die Industrie ihre Wettbewerbsfähigkeit.

→ Without innovation, industry loses its competitiveness.

💡 novus (new) is hiding inside 🚀 -tion sounds like ‘tsyohn’ — and it's always die.

Bonus innovativ = innovative

10

das maschinelle Lernen

[ma-shee-NEL-uh LAIR-nen]machine learning

(Sg.)

📌 Maschinelles Lernen wird bereits zur Früherkennung von Krankheiten eingesetzt.

→ Machine learning is already being used for the early detection of diseases.

💡 The Maschine learns = machine learning 🤓 A verb used as a noun is always das.

⚠ The adjective changes its ending: beim maschinellen Lernen

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

Many Germans care a lot about data protection (Datenschutz). When a new app or service comes out, the first question is often ‘Where does my data go?’, and websites tend to ask carefully for your consent.

Payments are becoming more digital, but plenty of people still prefer cash (Bargeld). In some small bakeries or shops, cash is easier than a card, so it's handy to keep a few coins in your wallet.

A fun fact: the Z3, often called one of the world's first programmable computers, was built by Konrad Zuse in Germany in 1941. 💻
