Today's words · 🐶 Pets October 24, 2026

Dogs, cats and more: 10 German pet words

A1A2B1B2

Meet a dog in a German park? Here's how to start chatting with its owner.

You're sitting on a park bench when a friendly dog trots over, wagging its tail. You want to ask if you can stroke it, but the words won't come, so you just smile. In Germany dogs are everywhere — on the U-Bahn, in cafés, in shops.

Today's ten words are all about pets. With them you can chat with dog owners, say yes when a neighbour asks you to feed their cat, and call the vet. 🐶

📋 The ten words at a glance

  1. das Haustier — pet
  2. der Hund — dog
  3. die Katze — cat
  4. das Kaninchen — rabbit
  5. füttern — to feed
  6. Gassi gehen — to walk the dog, take the dog out
  7. die Leine — lead, leash; (washing) line
  8. das Futter — (animal) feed, food; lining (of clothes)
  9. der Tierarzt — vet, veterinarian
  10. streicheln — to stroke, caress, pet

🧠 Words, examples and memory tricks

01

das Haustier

[HOUSE-teer]pet

(Pl. die Haustiere)

📌 Habt ihr ein Haustier?

→ Do you have a pet?

💡 Haus (house) + Tier (animal) = an animal that lives at home 🏠 Tier makes it das.

Bonus Do you have a pet? = Hast du ein Haustier?

02

der Hund

[HOONT]dog

(Pl. die Hunde)

📌 Der Hund spielt im Garten.

→ The dog is playing in the garden.

💡 Same root as English hound 🐕 The final d sounds like t.

Bonus to bark = bellen · a dog says Wau wau!

03

die Katze

[KAT-suh]cat

(Pl. die Katzen)

📌 Die Katze schläft auf dem Sofa.

→ The cat is sleeping on the sofa.

💡 English cat moved to Germany and became Katze 🐈 -e, so die.

⚠ A tomcat is der Kater — which also means a hangover!

04

das Kaninchen

[ka-NEEN-khen]rabbit

(Pl. die Kaninchen)

📌 Die Kinder füttern das Kaninchen mit Karotten.

→ The children are feeding the rabbit carrots.

💡 Ends in -chen, so it's a small, cute das 🐇

⚠ A pet rabbit is das Kaninchen; a wild hare is der Hase.

05

füttern

[FUE-tern]to feed

(füttert – hat gefüttert)

📌 Kannst du heute die Katze füttern?

→ Can you feed the cat today?

💡 Futter (food) + umlaut = to feed 🥣

Bonus With what: die Katze mit Trockenfutter füttern

06

Gassi gehen

[GAS-ee GAY-en]to walk the dog, take the dog out

(geht Gassi – ging Gassi – ist Gassi gegangen)

📌 Morgens gehe ich vor der Arbeit mit dem Hund Gassi.

→ In the morning I take the dog for a walk before work.

💡 Going out into the Gasse (lane) 🦮 Say ‘Gassi?’ and watch the tail go!

⚠ Perfect with sein: Ich bin mit dem Hund Gassi gegangen.

07

die Leine

[LY-nuh]lead, leash; (washing) line

(Pl. die Leinen)

📌 Im Park müssen Hunde an der Leine geführt werden.

→ In the park dogs must be kept on a lead.

💡 Same root as English line 🪢 A washing line is a Leine too.

Bonus to put a dog on the lead = den Hund anleinen

08

das Futter

[FOOT-er](animal) feed, food; lining (of clothes)

(no pl.)

📌 Hast du dem Hund schon Futter gegeben?

→ Have you fed the dog yet?

💡 A cousin of English food and fodder 🦴 Learn Futter and füttern as a pair.

⚠ Only for animals — food for people is das Essen.

09

der Tierarzt

[TEER-artst]vet, veterinarian

(Pl. die Tierärzte)

📌 Unsere Katze ist krank, wir müssen zum Tierarzt.

→ Our cat is ill, we have to go to the vet.

💡 Tier (animal) + Arzt (doctor) = animal doctor 🩺 Arzt makes it der.

Bonus A female vet is die Tierärztin.

10

streicheln

[SHTRY-kheln]to stroke, caress, pet

(streichelt – hat gestreichelt)

📌 Das Kind streichelt vorsichtig die Katze.

→ The child carefully strokes the cat.

💡 -eln adds a gentle, repeated feel 🤲 stroke, stroke, stroke.

Bonus Ask the owner: Darf ich ihn streicheln? (Can I stroke him?)

🇩🇪 A spoonful of German culture

You'll see dogs almost everywhere in Germany. Many shops, restaurants and buses and trains allow them, though it varies, so look for a sign at the door.

In most towns and cities, dog owners pay a dog tax (Hundesteuer). The amount and the rules depend on where you live, and so do the rules about when dogs must be kept on a lead.

If you want a pet, adopting from an animal shelter (Tierheim) is very popular. Shelters often have a chat with you first and sometimes check that your home is suitable. 🐾

✅ Quick quiz

Q. Your neighbour is going on holiday and needs someone to give the cat its food. What do they ask?

  1. ① Kannst du meine Katze streicheln?
  2. ② Kannst du meine Katze füttern?
  3. ③ Kannst du mit meiner Katze Gassi gehen?
Show the answer

② Kannst du meine Katze füttern?
füttern means to feed, so that's the word for giving the cat its food.
